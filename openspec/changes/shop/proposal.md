## Why

Today a member sees the gym's add-ons as one list of text rows at `/member/add-ons` ("Show at the desk"
selects an offer and creates nothing); the mobile member app lists no offers at all; products have no
picture, no grouping and no order. The v2 map makes "what a business does and sells" the front of the app.
The money machinery (`record_addon_sale`, payments, receipts, refunds, stock, `quote_version`, ADR-111/116,
`openspec/specs/addon-sales`) is blind-tested and **must not change**: SHP is a presentation and intent layer
over it. A member who wants a tub of protein can *reserve* it from the phone; the desk sees the reservation,
the member walks in and pays at the counter, and the desk turns the reservation into the existing sale in one
action. No online charging exists (ADR-146); the later PAY Buy tab (F10) supersedes and extends the
reservation (see "Forward compatibility" in ADR-181).

The MEDIA primitive exists because three v2 features need pictures and a private R2 bucket (`gymloop-media`,
ADR-006) with tenant-isolated keys, short-lived presigned URLs and a verified upload path is infrastructure,
not three features' worth of copies.

## Bar decisions and deliberate deviations

- **Shop is a view over `addon_products`, not a second catalogue.** The discriminator that already exists is
  `addon_products.kind` (enum `addon_kind`: `product`, `diet_plan`, `pt_package`). `product` → the Shop section
  "Products"; `diet_plan` → "Other services" (locker rent, towel service, day pass, guest pass — created in the
  existing editor as service offers; the enum label `diet_plan` is a Phase 6 name that no member ever sees);
  `pt_package` → never in the Shop (PTF). No enum value is added: every money trigger branches on these three
  labels and the contract forbids touching them.
- **Categories apply to `product` offers only; services are one flat list.** A per-tenant table because the
  map asks for rename and ordering.
- **Reservation = intent with an expiry, not a sale.** It moves no stock, creates no order, payment or receipt.
  The hold is *derived* (unexpired `reserved` rows are subtracted from stock when computing what another
  member may reserve) and it is a **soft hold**: a counter sale through the unchanged desk flow can still
  consume the last unit; the reservation then cannot be fulfilled (`GL057 insufficient_stock`, passed through
  untouched) and the desk cancels it with a reason the member sees.
- **Expiry has no sweeper.** `expires_at` is compared with `statement_timestamp()` at every decision (the INV
  precedent). A lapsed `reserved` row stays `reserved` forever, holds nothing, cannot change, and reads as
  `expired`. The enum therefore has no stored `expired` label.
- **Member reads go through definer RPCs, not table policies**: holds are other members' rows, images live in a
  table members cannot read, and the existing `addon_products_member_select` returns inactive and incomplete
  rows and stock (OPEN-015). That policy is left exactly as it is; the Shop does not depend on it.
- **Availability is a word, plus one capped quantity.** The RPC returns `available_quantity` (stock minus unexpired
  holds, floored at 0) so the stepper can be capped; the UI never prints it. No "only N left" threshold is
  invented.
- **GST is displayed as the gym's stored rate, never computed.** The money path charges `price_paise` exactly and
  computes no tax; the Shop shows the price, and when `gst_rate_bp > 0` one muted line "GST rate set by your
  {place}: 18%. You pay the price shown." It never says "includes" or "plus".
- **The cancel reason on a desk cancellation is shown to the member**, so the field is labelled
  "Reason (shown to the member)".
- **Expired reservations are never fulfilled or cancelled.** The desk simply sells normally through the existing
  flow; the old row ages out.

## EARS requirements

### MEDIA primitive (MED)

- **MED-001 (register).** WHEN real staff allowed for the kind (owner/manager for product/trainer; front office for announcement) call register_media_asset THE SYSTEM SHALL insert one unconfirmed/unattached tenant row with immutable staging key, caller creator, null published key/ETags, audit media_asset.registered and return id. Keys SHALL match `<tenant>/staging/<kind>/<uuid>.<ext>` with claim tenant, third segment kind and MIME-matching extension; published keys SHALL never register or be PUT-signed. Malformed/duplicate key or invalid MIME/bytes →22023; foreign tenant/forbidden actor/preview →42501; 61st tenant registration in rolling hour →GL086 media_limit, no write. Existing byte/role limits stay fixed.
- **MED-002 (trusted finalization).** WHEN the approved Edge verifier has authenticated a real allowed staff caller, verified staging and a fresh immutable published copy, and invokes service-only finalize_media_asset THE SYSTEM SHALL lock active staff then asset, recheck actor/tenant/kind/registration metadata and credential-only connection/request roles, apply the exact transaction-local finalization marker, stamp published key/source and published ETags/confirmed_at once and audit the real actor. Replay on eligible confirmed asset returns false with no write/audit; deleted →GL086 media_not_ready; actor/tenant mismatch →42501; malformed metadata →22023. Direct authenticated confirm/finalize SHALL be refused. Every invariant and marker shape in the approved verification section is binding, including postgres bypass refusal.
- **MED-003 (soft delete).** WHEN staff allowed for the asset's kind call `delete_media_asset` THE SYSTEM SHALL set `deleted_at`, write `media_asset.deleted`, and delete nothing physically; an attached asset SHALL fail `GL086 media_in_use` (it is released by its owner's command, MED-004); a replay on a deleted asset SHALL be a no-op; p_unconfirmed_only=true SHALL lock and no-op if already confirmed (invalid attempt cannot delete a concurrent publication); unknown or other-tenant SHALL fail `42501`.
- **MED-004 (attach and release protocol).** `app.media_attach` SHALL, for an asset of the stated tenant that is confirmed, undeleted and of the stated kind, set `attached_to_id` to the record id and write `media_asset.attached`; it SHALL fail `GL086 media_not_ready` for an unconfirmed or deleted asset, `GL086 media_kind_mismatch` for a different kind, `GL086 media_in_use` when the asset is attached to a different record, and `42501` for an unknown or other-tenant asset; re-attaching to the same record SHALL be a no-op. `app.media_release` SHALL, for the live asset attached to the stated record (if any), clear `attached_to_id`, set `deleted_at`, and write `media_asset.released` and `media_asset.deleted`. The partial unique index guarantees at most one live asset per record. The calling command, not these helpers, authorises the actor and validates that the record exists in the tenant; consumer commands are definer functions owned by `postgres`.
- **MED-005 (visibility and grants).** Staff/platform safe-column reads SHALL follow the media RLS policy; members/trainers/foreign tenants/anon SHALL read nothing. Authenticated SHALL receive no staging/published key or ETag SELECT, table-level SELECT or DML. Only the approved Edge service boundary SHALL read private metadata; only service_role SHALL execute finalize. Old confirm SHALL be inaccessible and always refused. Grant, owner, volatility and invariant-marker assertions SHALL match the fixed table and section below.
- **MED-006 (staging upload URL).** POST /api/media/upload-url SHALL derive a fresh staging key from verified tenant and server UUID, return PUT <=300s bound to content-type, and reject any published-key signing. It SHALL return no credential; the uploader cannot choose tenant/key. A reusable staging PUT SHALL never overwrite a confirmed published object.
- **MED-007 (verify, publish and races).** The Edge confirm operation SHALL HEAD staging, compare registered length/MIME, read magic bytes with source ETag If-Match, copy to a fresh unpredictable published key with CopySourceIfMatch, then HEAD/ranged-GET and recheck published length/MIME/magic and destination ETag before finalization. Missing/changed/mismatched/embedded-copy-error/ambiguous metadata SHALL never finalize. Concurrent confirms, actor revocation, delete, retries, uncertain finalizer outcome and exact orphan cleanup SHALL follow the approved section; no loser or timeout cleanup may remove the winning or ambiguously committed object.
- **MED-008 (Edge display URLs).** Before each GET <=900s the shared Edge signer SHALL check current caller-JWT feature exposure or permitted staff safe-row RLS, then privately resolve verified published key/MIME/ETag, tenant/kind/attachment/confirmed/undeleted state. Public member RPCs SHALL return only opaque image_asset_id, never key/MIME/ETag. No cached exposure or client key SHALL authorize signing; no web admin exists. Prior signed URLs MAY remain usable for their remaining TTL; no instant revocation claim. Bucket remains private.
- **MED-009 (tenant isolation).** Both namespaced keys SHALL use claim-derived owning tenant and correct kind, enforced by the fixed DB checks, register/finalize guards and Edge signing checks. The bucket-wide trusted R2 credential makes this application-level isolation, not per-tenant bucket policy. A tenant SHALL never register, expose, finalize or sign another tenant asset.
- **MED-010 (limits).** Allowed MIME types SHALL be exactly JPEG, PNG and WebP (no SVG, no GIF, no HEIC); the cap SHALL be 2 097 152 bytes; one image per record. Client-side pre-checks are permitted for usability and SHALL NOT be relied on.
- **MED-011 (audit).** Each MED command SHALL write `audit_log` through `app.media_audit`, attributed to `auth.uid()` and role for caller commands, and the independently verified real actor tuple for the service-only finalizer, `record_type = 'media_asset'`, `record_id` = the asset id, with no object key and no URL in `before`/`after`. Shapes: `registered` after `{kind, mime, bytes}`; `confirmed` before `{confirmed:false}` after `{confirmed:true}`; `attached` after `{kind, record_id}`; `released` before `{record_id}` after `{released:true}`; `deleted` before `{deleted:false}` after `{deleted:true}`.
- **MED-012 (retention).** Unconfirmed staging/orphan objects older than 7 days and soft-deleted objects 30 days after `deleted_at` are prunable; asset metadata tombstones remain for account-closure + 8-year configuration retention or as long as immutable version FKs refer to them, whichever is later, without cascade, SET NULL or history edits; no job is built (a `docs/security.md` row, like the other rows). The worst unattended growth is bounded by the registration limit (60 × 2 MiB per tenant per hour).
- **MED-013 (placeholder).** A record without a live image SHALL render a placeholder; a failed image load SHALL fall back to the same placeholder; the image SHALL carry an empty `alt` when the record's name is adjacent text.

### Shop (SHP)

- **SHP-001 (what the Shop lists).** THE SYSTEM SHALL list in the Shop exactly the offers for which `app.shop_offer_listable` is true: kind `product` (section Products) or `diet_plan` (section Other services), active, INR, with complete disclosed terms; `pt_package` offers, inactive offers, incomplete offers and non-INR offers SHALL never appear. An out-of-stock product SHALL appear with availability `out_of_stock`. Every listed offer SHALL be accepted by `record_addon_sale`'s completeness checks (stock aside).
- **SHP-002 (categories).** WHEN an owner or manager creates, renames, archives, restores or reorders categories THE SYSTEM SHALL apply the change through the `shop_categories` write policy only; names SHALL be unique per tenant ignoring case, 1–60 trimmed characters; a rename SHALL reach every product immediately (products reference the id); there is no delete. WHEN a category is archived its products SHALL read as uncategorised in the Shop and the console SHALL say so; an empty or archived category SHALL not appear to members. Categories apply to `product` offers only; services are uncategorised. Front desk, trainer, member and other tenants SHALL be unable to write (zero rows or `42501`).
- **SHP-003 (product display).** WHEN an owner or manager calls `set_shop_product_display` for a shoppable-kind product of their own tenant THE SYSTEM SHALL set `category_id`, `sort_order` and the product's image (attach, replace or remove through MED-004; a replaced or removed image is released and soft-deleted in the same transaction) and write `shop_product.display_changed` only if something changed. A category that is unknown, archived, of another tenant or given for a `diet_plan` SHALL fail `GL086 category_unavailable`; a `pt_package` product SHALL fail `GL086 item_unavailable`; `sort_order` outside 0…32767 SHALL fail `22023`; an unknown or other-tenant product, a non-admin role or an impersonator SHALL fail `42501`. The command SHALL NOT change price, stock, terms, activity or `quote_version`.
- **SHP-004 (member read surface).** WHEN a complete verified member session calls `read_member_shop` THE SYSTEM SHALL return one row per listable offer of the caller's own tenant with exactly the columns fixed above and no others — in particular no `stock_quantity`, trainer id, staff name, `sale_snapshot`, other member's data or hold count — ordered products before services, then by category (active categories by `sort_order`, then name; uncategorised last), then product `sort_order`, name, id, at most `catalogueMax + 1` rows. `category_id`/`category_name` SHALL be null for services and for an archived category; `image_asset_id` SHALL be non-null only for a confirmed, undeleted, `product`-kind asset attached to that offer; `availability` SHALL be `out_of_stock` when `stock_quantity` minus the unexpired holds is ≤ 0 for a product and `available` otherwise (services: always `available`); `available_quantity` SHALL be `max(stock_quantity − held, 0)` for a product and null for a service. Any caller who is not a complete member (anon, staff, platform, impersonator, member claim with a staff id, a user not bound to the claimed member) SHALL fail `42501`. Members SHALL hold no direct `select` on `shop_reservations`, `media_assets` or `shop_categories`.
- **SHP-005 (reserve).** WHEN a complete member session calls `create_shop_reservation` THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction, evaluate in exactly this order and stop at the first failure: caller (`42501`) → null argument (`22023`) → lock the product row (unknown or other-tenant id → `GL086 item_unavailable`) → member eligibility (`member_unavailable`) → `app.shop_offer_listable` (`item_unavailable`) → `quote_version` equals `p_quote_version` (`quote_changed`) → quantity ≥ 1, ≤ 10, exactly 1 for a service (`invalid_quantity`) → the member has no open reservation for this product (`reservation_exists`) → fewer than 5 open reservations and fewer than 10 created in the rolling 24 hours (`reservation_limit`) → for a product, `stock_quantity − held ≥ quantity` (`GL087 sold_out`). On success it SHALL insert one `reserved` row with the product name, kind, unit price, currency and quote version snapshotted and `expires_at = statement_timestamp() + 24 hours`, write `shop_reservation.created`, and return its id and expiry. It SHALL NOT create an order, payment, receipt or session, SHALL NOT change `stock_quantity`, and a refusal SHALL write nothing.
- **SHP-006 (holds and no overselling).** The quantity held for a product SHALL be the sum over its open reservations; reserving SHALL be refused rather than rounded or clamped when it would exceed stock minus holds; two concurrent reservations for the last unit SHALL produce exactly one row and one `GL087`; services SHALL hold nothing; lowering stock below the holds SHALL leave existing reservations standing and make new ones fail; a reservation SHALL never decrement stock — only the unchanged sale does, at acceptance, once.
- **SHP-007 (expiry).** A reservation SHALL be open only while `status = 'reserved'` and `expires_at > statement_timestamp()`; when `expires_at` is reached it SHALL stop holding, stop appearing as open, and read as `expired` (member and desk); no sweeper SHALL run; an expired row SHALL be unchangeable (fulfil and cancel fail `reservation_expired`).
- **SHP-008 (member cancel).** WHEN a member cancels their own open reservation THE SYSTEM SHALL set `cancelled_by_member`, `cancelled_at`, write `shop_reservation.cancelled`, and release the hold immediately. Another member's, another tenant's or an unknown id SHALL fail `42501` (indistinguishable); a fulfilled or cancelled one SHALL fail `reservation_not_open`; an expired one `reservation_expired`. Cancelling does not require a currently eligible member status.
- **SHP-009 (desk cancel).** WHEN real front-office staff cancel an open reservation of their tenant with a reason of 3–200 trimmed characters THE SYSTEM SHALL set `cancelled_by_gym`, `cancelled_at`, `cancelled_by_staff_id`, `cancel_reason`, and write `shop_reservation.cancelled` with the reason. A missing or too-short reason SHALL fail `22023`; trainers, platform users, impersonators and other tenants SHALL fail `42501`; terminal and expired reservations fail as in SHP-008.
- **SHP-010 (fulfil, atomic, money untouched).** WHEN real front-office staff call `fulfil_shop_reservation` for an open reservation THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction taking the reservation advisory lock first, verify the reservation is `reserved` and unexpired, call the **unchanged** `public.record_addon_sale` as the caller with the reservation's member, product and quantity, the supplied quote version, method, reason and idempotency key (trainer and slot arguments null), then call `app.shop_reservation_mark_fulfilled`, which SHALL re-derive and verify the caller (real front office), the reservation (own tenant, still open) and the order (same tenant, member, product and quantity; `sold_by_staff_id` equal to the caller; status `active` or `completed`; not linked to another reservation) before setting `fulfilled`, `fulfilled_at`, `fulfilled_by_staff_id`, `order_id` and writing `shop_reservation.fulfilled`. Any refusal of the sale — including `GL057 insufficient_stock`, `GL055 quote_changed`, `GL055 offer_unavailable`, `GL055 member_unavailable`, `GL052 idempotency_conflict` — SHALL roll back everything and leave the reservation `reserved`. Price, payment, receipt, GST, stock and audit rules are exactly the add-on sale's. A replay with the same idempotency key on an already-fulfilled reservation SHALL re-run `record_addon_sale` (returning its `replayed = true` result or its `GL052`) and write nothing new; a different key SHALL fail `reservation_not_open`. The price charged is the offer's current price; the desk sees any difference from the reserved price before confirming.
- **SHP-011 (state machine invariant).** `app.enforce_shop_reservation` SHALL bind every writer, including `postgres` and `service_role`: an insert SHALL be `reserved` with the fulfil and cancel columns null and `expires_at` after `created_at`; `id`, `tenant_id`, `member_id`, `product_id`, `product_name`, `kind`, `quantity`, `quote_version`, `unit_price_paise`, `currency`, `expires_at` and `created_at` SHALL be immutable; the only legal edges are `reserved → fulfilled`, `reserved → cancelled_by_member`, `reserved → cancelled_by_gym`, and only while `old.expires_at > statement_timestamp()` (otherwise `reservation_expired`); a terminal row SHALL be wholly immutable (`reservation_not_open`); `fulfilled` SHALL require an existing same-tenant order with the same member, product and quantity created at or after the reservation. Refusals use `GL086` with those DETAIL names.
- **SHP-012 (reservation visibility).** Front-office staff of the tenant SHALL read the tenant's reservations; trainers, other tenants and `anon` SHALL read none; a member SHALL read only their own through `read_member_shop_reservations`, which SHALL return exactly the columns fixed above, open reservations first (by expiry), then the most recent others, at most `MEMBER_PAGE_SIZE_DEFAULT` rows; `cancel_reason` SHALL be non-null only for `cancelled_by_gym`; `terms_changed` SHALL be true only for an open reservation whose product's current `quote_version` differs from the reserved one; `total_paise` SHALL be `unit_price_paise × quantity` computed as decimal text with no overflow and no floating point; `order_id` SHALL be non-null only for `fulfilled`.
- **SHP-013 (abuse limits).** No more than 5 open reservations and 10 creations per rolling 24 hours per member (`reservation_limit`, counted from `created_at`); one open reservation per member per product (`reservation_exists`); quantity ≤ 10.
- **SHP-014 (deactivated, deleted, history).** Deactivating an offer SHALL remove it from the Shop and from reservable items at once; open reservations on it SHALL stand until they expire but cannot be fulfilled (`offer_unavailable`), and the desk SHALL cancel them with a reason; reactivating SHALL resume their holds if unexpired. No offer is hard-deleted (no delete grant). Existing orders keep their `sale_snapshot`; changing a category or image SHALL never alter an order, a receipt or a reservation's snapshot columns. The desk and member lists SHALL show the reservation's own `product_name` snapshot.
- **SHP-015 (price and GST display).** Prices SHALL render from decimal text through the existing `formatMoney`, never through `Number`; a reservation SHALL show its snapshot unit price and a total of unit × quantity; the GST line is `shopGstLabel` (stored rate, informational, never a computed tax amount, never "includes" or "plus"); no screen SHALL show a GST amount.
- **SHP-016 (API).** The routes SHALL behave as in "Web": validate with the shared strict schemas, return the typed envelope, set `Cache-Control: no-store`, identify the caller before reading the body, map SQLSTATE/DETAIL by `Object.hasOwn` lookup, and never place an object key, signed URL or personal field in a log, error message or application query parameter (presigned image/upload URLs necessarily carry their temporary key/capability).
- **SHP-017 (console product manager).** The page SHALL behave as in "Web" and "States": owner/manager only for edits, front desk redirected, trainers denied, preview read-only; "N held for members" SHALL come from `read_shop_product_holds` and SHALL be labelled as a hold on member reservations, not as a stock reduction; price, stock and terms SHALL be editable only in `/add-ons`.
- **SHP-018 (console categories).** As SHP-002 plus the reorder command's atomicity: a failed reorder SHALL leave the previous order intact.
- **SHP-019 (console reservations).** Front office SHALL see open reservations soonest-expiring first, the reserved price beside the current price when the quote differs, and SHALL sell or cancel only through the two routes; the sell panel SHALL show the final amount from the current offer, require a method (or a reason for a zero price), reuse one idempotency key across retries, and on success link to the order and receipt; a refusal SHALL show the add-on sale's own refusal copy.
- **SHP-020 (member web Shop).** As "Web", "Shared" and "States": reserve in at most three taps; the confirm step states the `shopReserveNotice`; a refused reserve shows the pinned sentence and refreshes the item; the member's open reservations are listed first with Cancel; no purchase wording.
- **SHP-021 (mobile Shop).** As SHP-020 on the Shop tab, with `Sheet` confirmations, the last-good snapshot offline (SHP-022) and reserve/cancel disabled offline.
- **SHP-022 (offline).** WHEN the Shop loads successfully on mobile THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the last good response in memory for this app run only, without a new native dependency, scoped to the verified identity and show it, labelled with when it was saved, when the device is offline or a later load fails; reserve and cancel SHALL require a connection, SHALL NEVER be queued, and an offline attempt SHALL show a retry message with no fake success; the cache SHALL be cleared on sign-out or identity change; images already displayed MAY still render from the platform image cache, otherwise the placeholder shows. The web member pages are online-only.
- **SHP-023 (copy).** Every user-visible string SHALL be English, specific and honest, with no invented number; the pinned sentences in "Shared" SHALL be verbatim; place nouns SHALL come from `businessNouns`; no member screen SHALL say "order", "purchase", "bought" or "paid" about a reservation.
- **SHP-024 (tenancy and grants).** The three new tables SHALL be invisible across tenants; `authenticated` SHALL hold only the specified safe-column SELECT on media_assets, table SELECT on shop_reservations, SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE on shop_categories and no DELETE anywhere; `anon` SHALL hold nothing; the new definer functions SHALL carry an empty search path, be owned by postgres, and match their explicit execute grant: caller commands authenticated-only, finalize_media_asset service_role-only, private helpers postgres-only; never anon, and appear on the amended `04_contract_meta` allowlist at their declared volatility; `app.media_attach`, `app.media_release`, `app.media_audit`, `app.shop_audit` and `app.shop_held_quantity` SHALL be executable by no application role.
- **SHP-025 (audit).** Every reservation creation, fulfilment and cancellation and every display change SHALL write `audit_log` through `app.shop_audit`, attributed to `auth.uid()` and role, `record_type = 'shop_reservation'` (or `'shop_product'`), `record_id` the row id. Shapes: `created` after `{product_id, quantity, quote_version, expires_at}`; `fulfilled` before `{status:'reserved'}` after `{status:'fulfilled', order_id}`; `cancelled` before `{status:'reserved'}` after `{status:'cancelled_by_member'}` (role `member`) or `{status:'cancelled_by_gym'}` with `reason`; `display_changed` before and after `{category_id, sort_order, has_image}`. No token, URL or object key. The money events of the resulting sale are written by the existing money-audit triggers and are not duplicated.
- **SHP-026 (data lifecycle).** `shop_reservations` holds no personal data beyond the member link and the staff-typed `cancel_reason`; retention 1 year after the later of closure and expiry, erasable `blank` (clear `cancel_reason`); `media_assets` and `shop_categories` join the gym-configuration row (account closure, 8 years); product images contain no personal data.
- **SHP-027 (no regression).** `record_addon_sale`, `app.lock_addon_product_for_order`, `app.apply_addon_order_effects`, `app.stamp_addon_product_quote_version`, the add-on triggers, payments, receipts, refunds, stock rules, the desk sell flow at `/add-ons`, the order screens and every existing policy SHALL behave exactly as before; adding `category_id` to `addon_products` SHALL NOT rotate any `quote_version`; the desk sell flow SHALL still sell units a member has reserved (soft hold).
- **SHP-028 (forward compatibility with PAY).** No reservation column or label SHALL encode payment, proof or acceptance state. PAY MAY add states between `reserved` and `fulfilled` with `alter type … add value … before 'fulfilled'` and its own proof table keyed on `shop_reservations.id`, and MAY re-point acceptance to hold stock through the same product-row lock; `shop_reservations.order_id`, `quote_version` and the unit-price snapshot are the join points. The invariant trigger's edge list is the single place PAY extends.

## ADR-181 text

**ADR-181 — Shop with images, shared media, and member reservations (owner-approved feature map F3, 2026-10-02).** F3/SHP of the v2 map; it owns the MEDIA primitive of the batch-2 shared contract. Decisions, each with the alternative rejected. (1) *The Shop is a presentation layer over `addon_products`.* The discriminator is the existing `kind`: `product` → Products, `diet_plan` → Other services (locker rent, towel service, day and guest passes are service offers), `pt_package` → PTF. Rejected: a new `service` enum label (every add-on money trigger branches on the three labels, and the contract is "money rules untouched"), and a separate shop-products table (two catalogues, two prices). (2) *Categories are a per-tenant table* (rename and order are requirements); products only, services flat; archive instead of delete; an archived category reads as uncategorised. Rejected: a category enum (cannot rename or reorder per gym). (3) *Images live in the private R2 bucket behind a `media_assets` table; products reference nothing — the asset points at the product* (`attached_to_id`, one live asset per record by a partial unique index). The feature map's `image_url` column is rejected: a stored URL leaks tenancy, cannot be revoked and expires. The server, not the signature, decides what an upload is: the R2 documentation shows a signed `Content-Type` is enforced but does not document `Content-Length`, so the approved Edge verifier re-checks staging length/type/magic, ETag-conditionally copies to an immutable private published key and revalidates that copy; a service-only audited finalizer alone stamps confirmation. No reusable PUT can touch published keys. The R2 credential is bucket-wide; tenant isolation is the key prefix (derived from the claim, checked by the database, re-checked at signing) and is application-level. (4) *Members read through definer RPCs, not policies.* Availability needs other members' reservations, images need a table members cannot read, and the existing `addon_products_member_select` returns inactive and incomplete rows and stock (OPEN-015) — it is left untouched and unused. The RPCs return display fields only and one capped quantity; availability is a word, no "only N left" threshold is invented. (5) *A reservation is intent, not a sale.* It moves no stock and creates no money record. The hold is derived from unexpired rows and is soft: a counter sale through the unchanged desk flow can still take the last unit, in which case fulfilment is refused by the sale's own `insufficient_stock` and the desk cancels with a reason the member sees. Rejected: a strong hold, which needs `app.apply_addon_order_effects` and `app.lock_addon_product_for_order` to learn about reservations — an edit to the blind-tested money path for a convenience. (6) *Expiry is derived, there is no sweeper* (the INV precedent): a lapsed row stays `reserved`, holds nothing and cannot change; the enum has no `expired` label. (7) *Fulfilment goes through the unchanged `record_addon_sale`, as the caller, in one transaction with the reservation transition.* The sale function is `security invoker`; wrapping it in a definer would change the context the money triggers run in. The reservation write therefore happens in a definer helper that is executable by `authenticated` but unreachable through the Data API (schema `app` is not exposed), re-validates the caller, reservation and order itself, and is guarded by an invariant trigger that binds every writer. An advisory lock keyed on the reservation id serialises cancel and fulfil (an invoker cannot `FOR UPDATE` a table it cannot update); creation serialises on the product row like a sale. (8) *Forward compatibility with PAY (F10).* PAY's request is this reservation plus payment states: it adds enum labels before `fulfilled`, a proof table keyed on the reservation id, and moves the stock hold to owner acceptance through the same product-row lock; `order_id`, `quote_version` and the unit-price snapshot are the join points and no reservation column encodes payment. (9) *GST is displayed as stored, never computed*: the money path charges `price_paise` and computes no tax, so the Shop shows the rate as information and the price as the amount payable, and never claims "includes" or "plus". (10) *Errors*: `GL086` with DETAIL names for refusals (the `GL055` precedent), `GL087` for "not enough left"; the batch range is `GL086`–`GL087`. (11) *Offline*: in-memory only for this app run, no new native dependency; mobile shows the last good Shop read-only with a saved-at notice; reserving is never queued. (12) *Orphans and retention*: unconfirmed and deleted media objects are prunable on a stated schedule, with metadata tombstones retained for configuration/history integrity, no job built. Accepted decisions.md resolutions recorded here: `media_assets.attached_to_id`, tenant-leading object-key uniqueness, the `delete_media_asset` command and attach/release helpers, a registration rate limit, a magic-byte check on confirm, the client argument to `mediaDisplayUrl`, and owner/manager-only registration for kind `product`.

## Verification, races, replay and failure

1. Caller-scoped safe asset lookup, active allowed actor validation, then Edge-only privileged lookup of staging/verification fields; an already-confirmed undeleted allowed asset returns success after active-actor validation with no new copy or audit. It never returns success for deleted/invisible/ineligible assets.
2. HEAD staging, record source ETag, exact registered size and MIME; ranged GET with `If-Match` that ETag, verify format magic bytes. Missing -> upload_missing; invalid size/MIME/signature -> upload_rejected and request caller `delete_media_asset(assetId, true)` (object cleanup best effort). That command locks the asset and is a no-op if a concurrent verifier has already confirmed it, so a rejected attempt never deletes another attempt’s published winner; source conditional failure -> upload_changed (409, “The photo changed while it was being checked. Choose it again and retry.”), with no confirmation.
3. Generate a fresh unpublished destination UUID per verification attempt; HEAD must find no existing destination, otherwise choose another. Copy with `CopySourceIfMatch` of the verified ETag, preserving/checking registered Content-Type. No multipart upload (2 MiB cap). Source replacement after ranged check makes this conditional copy fail. A subsequent replacement of the staging object cannot modify the independent published copy. Record copy result destination ETag, HEAD and ranged GET the published object against that ETag and independently recheck size, MIME and magic bytes. Any missing ETag, embedded copy error, unexpected metadata or failed post-copy verification fails closed; no finalization.
4. Call finalizer with independently verified actor tuple and exact stored metadata plus source/destination ETags. Active staff revocation, deleted asset or altered stored registration before finalization causes refusal. Concurrent verifiers each create different candidates; locked finalizer admits one. Loser observes false, removes only its own unreferenced candidate, and reports success only after rechecking the winner's confirmed/undeleted eligible state. Never remove another attempt's candidate or the winning published object.
5. Finalizer timeout is an unknown outcome: revalidate caller through its safe RLS projection first, then Edge-only privileged reread for candidate-key comparison. If candidate key is the confirmed winning key, keep it and return success. If a definitive completed finalizer outcome and authoritative reread prove another candidate won or the row was deleted, clean only this positively unreferenced candidate. An unconfirmed reread alone cannot prove an in-flight finalizer will not commit: retain the candidate, return retryable storage_unavailable and reconcile via the same locked finalizer request or later orphan procedure before deletion. Failed/ambiguous reread means no deletion of candidate; return retryable storage_unavailable and retain an orphan for later object-only cleanup. Never interpret timeout as rollback. Successful replay emits no duplicate confirmation audit.
6. Staging cleanup is best effort after successful confirmation. Reusing a still-live staging PUT may recreate or alter only staging; confirmation replays never recopy it. Later object-only orphan pruning may remove residual staging after upload URL expiry plus the 7-day threshold. No cleanup job is built in this feature.

This supports the race design; it is not evidence that the deployed function or bucket passed verification. Live bucket conditional-copy behaviour must be demonstrated after approved deployment, before accepting upload UX.
