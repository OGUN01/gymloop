## Purpose, scope and dependencies

Deliver approved, minimally identifying renewal reminders, eligible absence follow-ups and receipt-availability notices through an official WhatsApp transport. Show the desk what needs attention and the owner what was actually accepted, delivered, read and charged. No two-way chat, arbitrary bulk campaigns, SMS, calling, gateway payment, automatic top-up or new identity binding. Receipt notices link to the existing authenticated receipt; WhatsApp text contains no balance, arrears, payment proof or receipt attachment. Existing click-to-WhatsApp is retained as an explicitly manual action; opening it remains neither delivery nor a paid send.

Comparable and evidence rubric: `docs/design/v2/wsp-bar.md`. Batch-2 GRD is an application/acceptance dependency, not assumed deployed truth. NTF's public proposal is NOTFROZEN and is used only to identify shared delivery seams. PAY receipt events will need a separately frozen causal source; WSP may initially consume only already-finalized existing payment/receipt records, never pending member proof as a receipt.

## Proposed EARS requirements

- **WSP-001 (authority).** WHEN a staff session requests dispatch THE SYSTEM SHALL admit only an active current-tenant front-office actor under the existing communications role boundary, refuse preview/impersonation writes, and accept only notification id plus request key. Tenant, recipient, category, cost, approval, provider id and result SHALL be derived by trusted code. Owner/manager alone configure templates/rates; trainer/member/platform support SHALL gain no dispatch or wallet mutation authority.
- **WSP-002 (recipient).** WHEN preparing and immediately authorizing provider I/O THE SYSTEM SHALL resolve current adult phone or current GRD guardian phone through `app.member_contact_phone`, reject erased/blocked/ineligible recipients, require the appropriate current versioned category consent AND WhatsApp-number consent, and refuse missing/ambiguous provenance. Unknown age follows GRD's fail-closed rules, not an inferred adult. Absence notices additionally require current scoring eligibility. The one-account-one-member contract SHALL remain unchanged; siblings sharing guardian phone remain separate member events and require consent scoped to each child and the actual recipient number.
- **WSP-003 (withdrawal/race).** WHEN consent is withdrawn or recipient/guardian/phone changes before the final dispatch authorization THE SYSTEM SHALL create no provider request and no debit, record `opted_out` using the current graph, release reservation and retain consent history. Preparation SHALL not freeze permission. The final ticket issuance and consent update SHALL serialize on the same member/consent boundary. Provider I/O SHALL start immediately on that ticket, never after its expiry; no guarantee of recalling an already initiated external request is advertised. A withdrawal committed after provider initiation stops future requests and does not erase truthful receipts or valid prior charges.
- **WSP-004 (templates).** WHILE a template is unapproved, paused, disabled, stale, recategorized or mismatched to its immutable revision/locale/parameter schema THE SYSTEM SHALL send nothing, charge nothing and expose the exact actionable template refusal. Rendered text SHALL use only approved bounded parameters (business name, optional first name, date and authenticated application link). No balance/arrears, health/absence score, token, proof image, contact list or credential SHALL enter message text. A receipt notice states availability, not a financial detail. Provider category changes invalidate rate authorization before another send.
- **WSP-005 (causal money).** WHEN a verified billable transport event matches a reserved attempt's tenant, sender account, provider id, template revision, price version and notification THE SYSTEM SHALL append exactly one negative ledger movement and derive the balance in the same transaction through the existing locked wallet path. Amount/currency SHALL come from the frozen server tariff/evidence, not webhook-supplied arbitrary values or a client field. Zero-cost evidence SHALL cause zero ledger rows because the existing ledger forbids zero movements. Invalid/non-INR/out-of-range costs SHALL fail closed. The server tariff SHALL be an approved final all-in INR integer-paise amount; THE SYSTEM SHALL perform no runtime tax or FX calculation. A sub-paise tariff requires a separately approved exact rounding amendment before activation; no undocumented rounding or floating-point conversion.
- **WSP-006 (funds).** WHILE the wallet is absent, empty or has less available paise than the approved maximum reservation THE SYSTEM SHALL initiate no paid provider request, show “WhatsApp not sent — wallet needs funds” with desk action, and retain the in-app event. Available amount SHALL be the one ledger-backed balance minus live holds. Concurrent reservation, wallet adjustment and finalization SHALL serialize on that wallet; two sends competing for the final amount cannot overspend. Holds SHALL not themselves alter balance or masquerade as a second wallet.
- **WSP-007 (acceptance/uncertainty).** WHEN a verified POST returns a provider id THE SYSTEM SHALL mark `sent` only as accepted-for-delivery, save exact causal evidence and preserve the applicable hold. WHEN rejection proves no accepted request THE SYSTEM SHALL mark failed and release the hold without charge. WHEN timeout/crash leaves acceptance unknown THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve an unresolved attempt/hold and show “Delivery outcome unknown”; it SHALL not automatically resend, debit, label delivered or release funds merely because a timer elapsed. Provider reconciliation or an explicitly reasoned authorized financial adjustment is required; inability to query by id does not prove rejection.
- **WSP-008 (receipts).** WHEN a verified provider receipt matches the sender and accepted attempt THE SYSTEM SHALL record its factual provider timestamp and idempotent evidence. `delivered_at` SHALL require delivery evidence. WhatsApp read evidence SHALL populate separate `provider_read_at`, never `clicked_at`; a click requires the existing authenticated acknowledgement. A read receipt may imply delivery but SHALL be applied through legal `scheduled→sent→delivered` steps with evidence, not by broadening the graph. Missing read receipt means unknown, never unread. Duplicates/out-of-order callbacks SHALL neither regress status nor debit again; evidence arriving after terminal failure SHALL be retained for reconciliation rather than illegally revive the notification.
- **WSP-009 (dedupe/fallback).** WHEN an event is replayed THE SYSTEM SHALL reuse the current tenant event/channel dedupe scheme and one live provider attempt; a request-key mismatch SHALL refuse rather than overwrite. Fallback SHALL have a separate causal `in_app` notification key referencing the same source and optional NTF push key; it SHALL not mutate the WhatsApp channel or charge a free fallback. Existing in-app source remains the source of truth, and no device token/NTF availability SHALL be assumed. Delivery failure SHALL surface desk/in-app fallback once; uncertainty SHALL carry honest copy, not an automatic duplicate paid send.
- **WSP-010 (audit/retention).** WHEN opt-in/out, template/rate activation, send refusal, reservation, acceptance, billable delivery, reconciliation or reasoned wallet adjustment occurs THE SYSTEM SHALL record actor/source, tenant, notification/attempt ids, consent/template/rate version references and allowlisted result metadata. Audit SHALL omit phone, rendered text, secrets and raw callback bodies. Existing eight-year wallet/audit/consent retention and one-year notifications retention SHALL remain; operational WSP records SHALL blank recipient-identifying metadata at one year and retain minimal non-PII ledger evidence for eight years. Erasure SHALL stop dispatch immediately; financial evidence SHALL survive without member PII. No unauthenticated receipt reveals member existence.
- **WSP-011 (limits/secrets).** WHEN a dispatch would exceed the frozen tenant cap or provider restrictions THE SYSTEM SHALL defer/refuse visibly without debit. Secrets SHALL remain in approved credential service/Vault custody, inaccessible to browser/mobile/table SELECT/logs; environment reads SHALL remain solely in registered env configuration. Verify raw webhook HMAC before parsing/mutating; challenge verification is not authentication of POST receipts. Bound request bytes, timestamp sanity and receipt cardinality; map tenant from the configured sender account, not incoming caller tenant ids.
