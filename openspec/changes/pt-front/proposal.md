# Personal training as a front section (PTF-001…PTF-034)

Feature F4 of `docs/planning/v2-feature-map.md`, phase V2-B3 of `docs/planning/v2-campaign-goal.md`, batch 2
(`openspec/changes/v2-batch2-shared/shared.md`, subordinate to `decisions.md`; migration `20261003130000_pt_front.sql`, tests
`73_pt_front.sql` / `h73_pt_front_holdout.sql`, ADR-182).
Rigor: **full blind arrangement (ADR-059; batch-2 decisions §8)** for the complete feature: separate visible DB, holdout DB, visible app and holdout app authors, all implementation-blind and blind to each other; a separate implementer never reads holdouts or changes tests. Contract and quality bar are frozen before authors start.

## Quality bar

The commissioned, fetched-reference bar is `docs/design/v2/ptf-bar.md`. It distinguishes official source observations from Gymloop acceptance requirements and is frozen before tests. No third-party visual proof or Indian-chain booking screenshot is claimed without a capture.

Measurable criteria the critic scores (structural, so they hold whatever the references turn out to say):
1. From the Training segment to a confirmed booking is at most four taps on a 390-wide screen (pack, day, time, Confirm).
2. The consequence of cancelling is stated, with the gym-local cutoff, **before** Confirm on the booking sheet and **before** the cancel button commits, in the exact sentences of `ptCancellationConsequence` and `ptBookingConsequence` (Shared), which are the rule the database applies (parity-tested at the cutoff instant).
3. Every refusal names what happened and the next action in one sentence and asserts no cause the system does not know (the pinned PTF refusal sentences are pinned).
4. The slot list shows only times that will be accepted; a taken time is absent, not greyed; an empty list says why in one line (no availability, time off, or nothing open in the booking window) and offers the next action.
5. Status is dot plus word, with these words only: Booked, Attended, No-show, Cancelled by you, Cancelled by your {place}, Cancelled late - session used.
6. Slots are buttons with accessible names that include the weekday, date and time; 44 px targets; reduced motion; light and dark; large text; axe-clean at 390 and 1440.
7. Times render in the trainer's branch timezone (gym timezone when the branch has none), and the zone is named when it differs from the device's.
8. Console: a trainer with three weekly windows is saved without leaving the page; overlapping windows are refused inline before submit and again by the database.

## Why

Today personal training is an add-on: the desk sells a pack (`record_addon_sale`, which requires an initial slot),
and only the assigned trainer may schedule or finish sessions (`schedule_pt_session`, `finish_pt_session`). A
member can look at their sessions and nothing else; there is no trainer profile, no stated availability, no way
to book, and nothing to do when a trainer leaves with unspent packs. PTF makes training a front section of the
app: members see trainers and their programmes, book sessions on a trainer's stated availability, and cancel
under the gym's stated rule; staff manage profiles, availability, reassignment and policy. Every session stays a
`pt_sessions` row and every consumed session stays in `addon_orders.sessions_used`, so the blind-tested money
rules (ADD-004…012) are reused, not rebuilt.

## Design decisions and deliberate deviations

- **D1 — `pt_sessions` is the booking.** No parallel booking table with a mirrored status. A member booking inserts a `pt_sessions` row (status `scheduled`) against the member's own active pack, so the exclusion constraint `pt_sessions_trainer_overlap_excl`, the reservation budget, the order lock and every Phase 6 reader see it at once. `public.booking_status` (CLS-owned) is the **read vocabulary** the PTF reads return: `scheduled`→`booked`, `completed`→`attended`, `no_show`→`no_show`, `cancelled`→`cancelled_by_member` or `cancelled_by_gym` from the provenance row (absent row = `cancelled_by_gym`: a trainer's own cancel through `finish_pt_session` and the refund-driven cancel are both gym-side).
- **D2 — Availability is the simplest thing that supports the flow.** Weekly recurring windows per trainer (weekday, start minute, end minute) + whole-day time off ranges + **one gym-wide session length** (`pt_session_minutes`, default 60). A member picks from on-grid start times derived on read; there are no pre-generated slot rows to go stale or to lock. The duration of a member-booked session is server-derived; the client sends only a start.
- **D3 — Late-cancel consumption is a separate counted outcome recorded in the order's own counter, with provenance.** The `pt_sessions` row of a late-cancelled session stays `cancelled` (identity, slot and notes permanent; the legal transition set unchanged; no `completed` row is faked). If the gym's `pt_late_cancel_consumes_session` is true the cancel command, in the same transaction and under the order lock, adds one to `addon_orders.sessions_used` and records a `pt_cancellations` row with `consumed = true`. Rationale: `sessions_used` stays the **single** place every reader (member add-ons page, mobile snapshot, console order detail, refund logic, TRV) already looks, so no reader can forget to subtract a second ledger; the rule and the audit trail are in `pt_cancellations`. ADD-007's last clause is amended (PTF-023) — owner-approved on 2026-10-02. Rejected: a derived "forfeited" count joined by every reader (six silent-overstatement sites), and consuming through a fake `completed` row (breaks ADD-007's "completion happens after the slot ends").
- **D4 — PT has its own cancel window column, `pt_cancel_window_hours` (default 24), not CLS's `class_cancel_window_hours`.** Deviation from `shared.md` ("same window value unless you justify otherwise"). Justification: (a) the PTF migration sorts before CLS's, so a reference to a CLS column cannot exist at apply time or in PTF's own pgTAP sweep; (b) a one-to-one slot cannot be refilled at short notice, so the right window for training differs from a class's. The policy flag stays `pt_late_cancel_consumes_session` as `shared.md` fixes it.
- **D5 — Member commands are admitted into the Phase 6 PT guards by a command setting, not by loosening them.** Three Phase 6 triggers/functions refuse every authenticated context that is not a real staff session (verified from migration `20260915100004`): `app.lock_addon_order_for_pt_session`, `app.addon_order_fully_returned`, and, for reassignment, the frozen-terms check of `app.enforce_addon_order`. PTF amends the first two only for their stated member-command shapes, and `enforce_addon_order` only for the named reassignment and owner-approved completed-pack-waiver shapes, each keyed on a transaction-local setting set by a `postgres`-owned PTF command (the `app.staff_binding_command` precedent of STI/ADR-176); PTF-023 states precisely why a forged setting is harmless, including the one place (the definer order-lock trigger) where `current_user` cannot be the argument. Rejected: blanking `request.jwt.claims` around the insert (breaks attribution and every claim-reading trigger), and a second pt_sessions-like table.
- **D6 — Trainer self-service is bounded.** An active linked trainer edits their own bio, specialities, availability and time off; owners and managers edit all of it and alone control the photo and the `is_listed` flag (a profile is not shown to members until the owner lists it). The orchestrator retains bounded trainer self-service.
- **D7 — The trainer's "own clients only" is enforced on every PTF surface, not by narrowing the Phase 2 tables.** The new tables and RPCs scope a trainer to their own rows in the database. The pre-existing `pt_sessions`/`addon_orders` trainer read breadth (OPEN-015 family) is **not** changed here: narrowing it touches ten existing suites and would still leave `members` readable by trainers, so it is stated honestly in `docs/security.md` instead of half-done.
- **D8 — Purchase stays at the desk.** Programmes are the existing active PT offers shown as training programmes. PTF adds no purchase, request or payment path (ADR-146, PAY).
- **D9 — Migration order is a hard precondition (R1).** PTF consumes `public.booking_status` and `app.booking_lock`, and `app.member_has_live_membership`, provided by CLS-owned `…085000_booking_primitives.sql`. They must exist before `…130000`. See Requires; both features consume the early primitives migration.
- **D10 — Policy at cancellation time applies.** The window and flag **in force when the member cancels** decide the outcome and are frozen into the `pt_cancellations` row; a policy change never rewrites history. The booking sheet and the session row always show the cutoff computed from the current policy.

## EARS requirements

**Profiles and the member-safe trainer read**

- **PTF-001 (profile authority).** WHEN a real gym owner or manager sets a trainer's profile (bio of at most 600 characters after trim; at most 8 specialities of 1..40 trimmed characters, unique ignoring case; an optional photo; a listed flag) for an active `staff` row of role `trainer` in their own gym THE SYSTEM SHALL upsert exactly one `trainer_profiles` row for that staff member and write `trainer_profile.updated`. WHEN an active, linked trainer sets their own bio and specialities THE SYSTEM SHALL change only those two columns of their own row (creating it unlisted when absent) and SHALL never change `is_listed` or `photo_asset_id`. Front desk, member, platform, an impersonator, another trainer, another gym and an unknown staff id SHALL be refused `42501`; a staff row whose role is not `trainer` SHALL fail `22023` (`not_a_trainer`); an invalid bio or speciality `22023` (`bio_invalid`, `speciality_invalid`).
- **PTF-002 (photo).** A photo SHALL be a `media_assets` row of the caller's tenant with `kind = 'trainer'`, confirmed and not deleted (else `22023` `photo_unavailable`); `null` clears it; the profile stores only the asset id, never a URL or object key. Only owner or manager may set or clear it. WHEN the photo changes THE SYSTEM SHALL, in the profile transaction, release the old attachment through SHP’s `app.media_release` and attach the new confirmed same-tenant asset through `app.media_attach`, preserving `attached_to_id`; a failure rolls back the profile change. The composite `(tenant_id, photo_asset_id)` FK references `media_assets(tenant_id,id)`. Members obtain display URLs only through SHP’s `POST /api/member/media-url` `{ assetId }`, which rechecks PTF-003 visibility; no PTF photo route or schema is added. The console states beside the upload: "Only add a photo your trainer has agreed to show members." (DPDP).
- **PTF-003 (member trainer read).** `read_member_trainers` SHALL return, for a complete member identity, one row per **active** `trainer` of the member's gym that either has a listed profile or is the assigned trainer of an active, complete PT offer; `bio`, `specialities` and `is_profile_listed = true` only when the profile is listed, else empty/false; `image_asset_id` SHALL be non-null only when the profile is listed and its trainer-kind image is confirmed, undeleted and currently attached to this same-tenant staff row; otherwise image_asset_id is null; `qualification` from `staff.qualification`; `branch_name` null for all-branch trainers. It SHALL expose no staff id, user id, email, phone, role, branch id or `is_active`, and never a row of another gym, an inactive trainer, or a trainer with neither a listed profile nor an active offer. The trainer is identified only by `trainer_key`. The public member RPC SHALL expose only image_asset_id, never storage key/MIME/ETag. `memberMediaUrl(supabase,assetId)` SHALL forward original caller JWT to SHP’s approved Edge media member-url operation, which rereads current trainer exposure as caller, then privately checks same-tenant trainer kind, confirmed/undeleted published asset and canonical app.pt_trainer_key(verifiedTenant, asset.attached_to_id) equality with the returned trainer_key for that same image_asset_id before GET signing; trainer_key SHALL never be compared directly to attached_to_id. Web/mobile rendering envelopes contain imageUrl only; no web admin client or end-user key projection exists. The generic `POST /api/member/media-url` SHALL accept only `{ assetId }`, repeat this current exposure check and return `data: { imageUrl: string }`; callers supply no tenant/key/MIME. Anything but a complete member identity (including an impersonator, staff and platform) SHALL be refused `42501`.
- **PTF-004 (programmes).** `read_member_programmes` SHALL return the member's gym's `addon_products` of kind `pt_package` that are active, have complete disclosure (description, cancellation terms, validity, trainer qualification, session count, no stock) and an active `trainer` assigned, with the exact price as decimal text (`price_paise`), currency, `gst_rate_bp` as stored, session count, validity days, the disclosed trainer qualification and cancellation terms (ADD-002). Inactive or incomplete offers SHALL never appear. THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT pre-select a programme (ADD-001) and SHALL create no order, request or payment from the app; the action offered is the existing "Show at the desk" pattern, and nothing is sold or reserved by selecting. Price and GST render exactly as the existing offer card renders them; no tax arithmetic is introduced.
- **PTF-005 (pack read model).** `read_member_pt_packs` SHALL return the member's own PT orders in status `active`, `completed` or `refunded` with the frozen programme name (`sale_snapshot->>'name'`, product name for legacy rows), `sessions_total`, `sessions_used` as recorded on the order, `sessions_scheduled` (count of its `scheduled` sessions), `sessions_remaining = max(total - used - scheduled, 0)` except for expired packs, which use `max(total - used, 0)` under the owner-approved PTF-018 expiry exception, validity dates, `state` (`pt_pack_state`) and `can_book`. `can_book` is true only when the state is `live`, the pack's trainer is an active `trainer`, the trainer's branch is null or equals the member's, and the member has a live membership today; it is a convenience — `book_pt_session` re-decides everything. `read_pt_packs` is the staff view of the same facts (PTF-032).

**Availability**

- **PTF-006 (windows).** WHEN an owner or manager (any trainer of their gym) or an active linked trainer (themselves only) replaces a trainer's weekly windows THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction, delete that trainer's rows and insert the supplied ones (at most 28; `weekday` 0..6; `0 <= start_minute < end_minute <= 1440`; no two windows of one weekday overlap) and write `trainer_availability.replaced`. An empty array clears availability (no time is then offered). Overlap or out-of-range input SHALL fail `22023` (`window_overlap`, `window_invalid`) and write nothing; the exclusion constraint is the backstop. Changing windows SHALL NOT alter any existing `pt_sessions` row.
- **PTF-007 (time off).** WHEN the same actors add time off (`starts_on <= ends_on`, optional reason of at most 200 characters) or remove their own previously added entry THE SYSTEM SHALL record it (removal sets `removed_at`, never deletes) and write `trainer_time_off.added` / `trainer_time_off.removed`; a removal replay is a no-op. Time off SHALL only stop **new** offers; sessions already booked inside the range stand until a staff cancel or reassignment, and the console lists them beside the entry.
- **PTF-008 (slot state, one rule for read and write).** `app.pt_slot_state(tenant, trainer, starts_at)` SHALL return the first matching state of: `too_soon` (`starts_at < statement_timestamp() + 60 minutes`), `beyond_horizon` (`starts_at >= statement_timestamp() + 28 days`), `holiday` (the gym-local date, in the trainer's timezone, is in `organization_holidays`), `time_off` (inside a non-removed entry), `not_offered` (not exactly on a grid start: whole minutes with zero seconds, the local minute of day `m` satisfying `start_minute <= m`, `m + L <= end_minute` and `(m - start_minute) mod L = 0` for some window of that weekday, with `L = pt_session_minutes`), `taken` (an overlapping `scheduled` or `completed` session of that trainer, any member), else `open`. `read_member_pt_slots` SHALL return exactly the `open` starts of the member's own pack's trainer within the requested inclusive gym-local date range (at most 14 days; wider or inverted → `22023` `range_invalid`), clamped to the pack's validity, ascending, at most 400 rows, and zero rows (never an error) when `can_book` (PTF-005) would be false for that pack or the order is not the member's, so the screen's one gate and its list always agree. THE SYSTEM SHALL guarantee that a start returned by the read is accepted by `book_pt_session` unless a member-specific refusal (membership, overlap, rate limit, branch) or a concurrent booking intervenes.

**Booking**

- **PTF-009 (booking eligibility and check order).** WHEN a complete member identity calls `book_pt_session` THE SYSTEM SHALL decide in this order, first failure wins: identity (`42501`); null arguments (`22023`); the order is the member's own PT order (else `42501`, indistinguishable from unknown); locks (the lock-order row); trainer unchanged since the first read (else `40001`); replay (PTF-011); throttle (`GL097`, PTF-012); member bookable and `app.member_has_live_membership` on the session's gym-local day (`GL093`); pack — order `active`, validity dates present, not fully returned (`GL055` `order_unavailable`), pack trainer an active `trainer` (`GL055` `trainer_unavailable`), trainer's branch null or the member's (`GL094`), `sessions_used + scheduled < sessions_total` (`GL058` `session_budget_exhausted`), the whole slot inside `starts_on..expires_on` (`GL058` `session_outside_validity`); the member has no `scheduled` PT session overlapping the slot with any trainer (`GL091`); `app.pt_slot_state` — `taken` is `GL096`, every other non-`open` state `GL092` with the state in `detail`; then the insert.
- **PTF-010 (double-booking and the race).** `book_pt_session` SHALL take `app.booking_lock(tenant, 'trainer_slot', trainer staff id)` before it counts anything, so two members racing for one trainer's slot get exactly one booking and the other `GL096`. A `23P01` from `pt_sessions_trainer_overlap_excl` at insert (the only way to lose a race against the unlocked trainer-side `schedule_pt_session`) SHALL surface as `GL096`. The database SHALL never hold two `scheduled`/`completed` sessions of one trainer overlapping in time, whatever the writer.
- **PTF-011 (the write, idempotency, admission).** THE SYSTEM SHALL insert exactly one `pt_sessions` row: `id = p_session_id`, tenant, order, member and trainer taken from the order, `ends_at = starts_at + pt_session_minutes`, status `scheduled`, `notes` null, through the admitted member-command shape of PTF-023, then write `pt_session.booked`. A retry presenting an existing session id with the same order, member, trainer and start SHALL return that row with `replayed = true` and write nothing (its current `status` is returned, which may since have changed); any other fact under the id SHALL fail `GL052`. A booking never creates attendance; it is not a check-in.
- **PTF-012 (throttle).** THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse `GL097` a booking when the member's Auth user has 10 or more `pt_session.booked` audit rows in the rolling 24 hours; the check follows replay so a legitimate retry is never throttled; a refusal writes nothing.

**Cancellation and consumption**

- **PTF-013 (member cancellation).** WHEN the session's own member calls `cancel_pt_booking` on a `scheduled` session whose `starts_at` is later than `statement_timestamp()` THE SYSTEM SHALL, under the order lock, set its status to `cancelled` through the admitted shape (PTF-023), insert one `pt_cancellations` row (`outcome = cancelled_by_member`, `window_hours` and `policy_consumes` as currently set, `was_late = statement_timestamp() > starts_at - window_hours hours`, `consumed = was_late and policy_consumes`) and write `pt_session.cancelled`. Cancelling at exactly the cutoff instant is not late. A session whose `starts_at` is not later than now SHALL fail `GL095`. An already `cancelled` session is a replay (`replayed = true`, no write); `completed` or `no_show` fails `GL058` (`invalid_session_transition`); another member's or an unknown session `42501`. The freed slot is bookable again at once.
- **PTF-014 (late cancel is a separate counted outcome — amends ADD-007).** WHEN a cancellation is `consumed` THE SYSTEM SHALL, in the same transaction and under the order lock, add one to the order's `sessions_used` and, when that makes `sessions_used = sessions_total`, move the order `active → completed` (a legal transition; no scheduled session can remain, because `used + scheduled <= total` always holds). The cancelled `pt_sessions` row, its identity, slot and notes SHALL be unchanged, no `completed` row SHALL be created, ADD-004 (never below zero) and the `addon_orders_sessions_used_within_total_chk` check hold, and the existing order audit trigger records the usage change (ADD-012) in addition to `pt_session.cancelled`. Invariant, asserted in pgTAP over every order created and closed after this migration: **`sessions_used` = the order's `completed` sessions + its `pt_cancellations` with `consumed` and `waived_at is null`**; legacy orders and fixtures that set `sessions_used` directly are exempt. WHEN this cancellation sees a locked active PT order with `sessions_used = sessions_total - 1` and consumes its last session THE SYSTEM SHALL record `completed_order = true` in that same cancellation transaction; every other cancellation records false, and the marker remains immutable through waiver and later commands. A `no_show`, a gym or trainer cancellation, and a refund-driven cancellation SHALL consume nothing, exactly as before.
- **PTF-015 (gym cancellation).** WHEN an owner, manager or front-desk member cancels a `scheduled` session with a reason of 3..200 trimmed characters THE SYSTEM SHALL set it `cancelled`, insert a `pt_cancellations` row (`cancelled_by_gym`, `was_late` false, `consumed` false), write the `pt_session_cancelled_by_gym` notice (PTF-025) and `pt_session.cancelled_by_gym`; it consumes nothing. A replay returns `replayed = true`; a `completed`/`no_show` session fails `GL058`. A trainer's own cancel stays the existing `finish_pt_session` (no provenance row; the member's list shows `cancelled_by_gym`).
- **PTF-016 (waive — owner-approved completed-pack exception, 2026-10-02).** WHEN a complete real owner or manager calls `waive_pt_forfeit` for a same-tenant consumed member late cancellation with a trimmed reason of 3–200 characters THE SYSTEM SHALL take the session advisory lock, then order advisory lock, then order/session/cancellation row locks in the existing PTF order, and revalidate under those locks. An active order follows the existing waiver rule. A completed order SHALL be eligible only when its cancellation has `completed_order = true`, `was_late`, `policy_consumes`, `consumed = true`, no waiver, the session remains cancelled, the keys/member/trainer match the order and provenance, the PT order has `sessions_used = sessions_total`, no scheduled reservation remains, and the original validity includes the current command's gym-local day. An expired, refunded, fully returned or partially returned pack SHALL be refused GL055 `order_unavailable`; any recorded return for this order excludes the completed-pack exception. Missing/null validity excludes it. Ordinary completed packs or completions caused by attendance SHALL be refused GL055 unchanged. An eligible completed-pack waiver SHALL decrement used by exactly one, set all three existing waiver fields, change completed → active, and write the existing waiver audit/notice atomically. **Reassignment, expiry, policy**

- **PTF-017 (reassign).** WHEN an owner or manager reassigns PT packs from one trainer to another with a reason of 3..200 characters (explicit ids, or all of the source's `active` PT orders when `p_order_ids` is null; at most 100 or `22023` `batch_too_large`) THE SYSTEM SHALL, atomically for the whole batch and under the PTF lock order: refuse `GL055` `trainer_unavailable` if the target is not an active `trainer` of the gym; `22023` `same_trainer` if source equals target; `42501` for an order that is unknown, of another gym, not PT, or not assigned to the source; `GL055` `order_unavailable` for an order not `active`; `GL094` if the target's branch differs from any listed member's; then, per order, cancel **all** its `scheduled` sessions (provenance rows `cancelled_by_gym`, no consumption — including past slots the source never closed), change `addon_orders.trainer_staff_id` through the admitted shape (PTF-023), write one `pt_pack_reassigned` notice per order and `pt_order.reassigned`. ## ADR-182 text

**ADR-182 — Personal training as a front section: member booking on the existing PT ledger (batch 2, 2026-10-02).** F4/PTF of the v2 map. Decisions, each with the alternative rejected. (1) *`pt_sessions` is the booking.* A member booking inserts a `scheduled` `pt_sessions` row against their own active pack, so the exclusion constraint, the reservation budget, the order lock and every existing reader apply to it unchanged; `public.booking_status` is the read vocabulary derived from `pt_session_status` plus a provenance row. Rejected: a parallel `pt_bookings` table with a mirrored status — it needs a sync trigger over three writers (trainer finish, refund cancel, member cancel) and drifts silently. (2) *Availability is weekly windows + time off + one gym-wide session length*, evaluated on read by one function (`app.pt_slot_state`) that the booking command also calls, so the list and the decision cannot disagree. Rejected: pre-generated slot rows (stale, lockable, backfill-prone) and per-trainer durations (a grid per trainer for no demonstrated need). ## SQL availability JSON encoding (orchestrator clarification, 2026-10-02)

`public.set_trainer_availability(p_staff_id uuid, p_windows jsonb)` receives the
same strict window shape as `trainerAvailabilityRequestSchema`, without a second
adapter-specific vocabulary. `p_windows` is a JSON array of objects whose exact
keys are `weekday`, `startMinute`, `endMinute`. All three values are JSON numbers
with the contracted integer/range/window checks; numeric strings, booleans,
null/missing values, snake_case aliases and unknown keys are malformed input.
SQL NULL, non-array input and non-object elements are likewise refused with
22023, after existing actor authorization. An empty array removes availability;
the existing 28-window limit, atomic replacement, overlap, audit and permissions
remain unchanged. Stored/audited database row fields retain `start_minute` and
`end_minute`; they do not define the command's JSON encoding.

This closes an omitted interface encoding under the orchestrator's standing
delegation; it adds no new column, function, route, permission or business rule.
Both independent DB authors were paused before this wording was fixed. The
recorded-return boundary in `completed-pack-return-boundary.md` was subsequently
approved by the owner: the completed-pack exception is excluded by ANY
`public.refunds` row with the order's tenant_id and payment_id, without a status,
amount or currency filter. Requested, processing, completed and failed rows all
exclude it; unrelated tenant/payment rows do not. This same predicate applies to
both PTF-016 revalidation and PTF-023's exact completed→active guard exception.
Ordinary full-return accounting and active-pack waiver rules stay unchanged.
Fresh review precedes resuming either author.
