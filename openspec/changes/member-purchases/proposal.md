# Member purchase requests and private payment claims (PAY)

**DRAFT — NOT FROZEN. 2026-10-03.** Public contract preparation only. No approval, test, implementation, migration, deployment or acceptance is claimed. Existing PAY-001…012 remain authoritative; this feature uses **BUY-001…025** to avoid reusing their IDs. ## Purpose and bar

A member requests a Shop item, PT programme or renewal of their current plan. Real front-office staff accept the request, then verify externally received money and record it through the existing ledger. A screenshot is the member's claim, never bank verification, payment, receipt or entitlement. No gateway, webhook, UPI intent, credentials, OCR verdict or automatic settlement is added. Plain counter sales remain first class.

`docs/design/v2/pay-bar.md` supplies fetched references and measurable own criteria. Money, identity, every policy/grant, proof binding and expiry require separate implementation-blind visible and holdout authors and fresh critics (ADR-059). Screen work may use relaxed rigor only after that contract is frozen.

Renewal names the held membership and its existing plan only. Another plan routes to the desk. Replace only PLC's explanatory `deskNote` when PAY ships.
- MEDIA: approved immutable staging → conditional verification → published object protocol, `matchesImageSignature`, MIME/extension mappings and existing Edge verifier/signer. Current `media_assets` requires staff creator, exposes safe metadata to staff, permits only product/trainer/announcement and signs public feature exposure. **Payment proof is presently unsupported**: extending kind, actor model, permission gates and private signer is part of PAY, not permission to use the existing public-photo route.
- NTF: transactional request events use its existing category/consent/quiet-hour defaults and dedupe semantics. F10's transactional classification does not waive a delivery consent already required by NTF. In-app request truth does not depend on successful push.
- Central IA remains **Home · Classes · Shop · Activity · You**. “Buy / Payments” is a dedicated destination `/member/buy` and mobile `MemberBuy`, opened from Shop item “Request”, Classes → Training programme “Request”, Gym held-plan “Request renewal”, Home renewal action and You “Buy / payments”. ## EARS requirements

- **BUY-001 (actor).** WHEN a real member initiates a request THE SYSTEM SHALL derive tenant/member/Auth identity from canonical claims and revalidate the live binding and gym eligibility, refusing blocked, cancelled, erased or pending-status self-service. WHEN front office acts THE SYSTEM SHALL revalidate an active staff row matching Auth subject, tenant, staff id and canonical owner/manager/front-desk role. Trainers, platform preview and impersonation SHALL NOT mutate or view proof.
- **BUY-002 (request).** WHEN an eligible member confirms a currently exposed Shop offer, programme or held-plan renewal THE SYSTEM SHALL persist one typed intent and disclosed server snapshot, with current quote/revision, integer-paise decimal text at the API, explicit currency and stored GST rate where present. Quantity SHALL obey SHP rules; PT and renewal quantity SHALL equal one. Browser amounts, tax, tenant, seller and eligibility SHALL NOT be authoritative.
- **BUY-003 (scope).** THE SYSTEM SHALL accept only an existing SHP-listable offer, PTF-listable programme or the member's current eligible held membership/plan; inactive plans, arbitrary membership ids, other plans and cross-tenant targets SHALL NOT be purchasable. Complimentaries stay the existing desk path; PAY SHALL NOT invent a zero-money screenshot payment.
- **BUY-004 (accept).** WHEN front office accepts a requested intent THE SYSTEM SHALL lock request and the existing resource locks, recheck price, stock and eligibility, persist accepted disclosed terms and expiry, and audit atomically. Changed price SHALL require an explicit member reconfirmation of the revised quote before proof can be submitted. Acceptance SHALL create no paid row, receipt, membership extension or usable pack.
- **BUY-005 (state).** THE SYSTEM SHALL use database enums for `requested`, `owner_accepted`, `payment_proof_uploaded`, `recorded`, `rejected`, `cancelled`, `expired`; proof disposition SHALL separately use `active`, `superseded`, `rejected`, `bound`. Requested→accepted/rejected/cancelled/expired; accepted→proof-uploaded/cancelled/expired; proof-uploaded→accepted (reasoned rejection)/recorded/cancelled/expired. Replacement retains proof-uploaded. Recorded and other terminal states SHALL NOT reopen. Proof rejection is not request rejection.
- **BUY-006 (cancel).** WHEN the owning member cancels any unrecorded open request THE SYSTEM SHALL serialize against recording, close it and release its existing hold atomically, including after acceptance or upload. A cancelled request SHALL never later activate a purchase. Cancellation needs ownership, not current purchase eligibility.
- **BUY-007 (expiry).** WHILE expiry has passed or the member is unavailable THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse acceptance, proof attachment and recording, show Expired, and release effective stock capacity without requiring a successful sweeper. A service-only expiry command SHALL materialize closure and its audit exactly once; reads SHALL NOT write. Cancellation/recording/expiry races SHALL have one winner and no leaked hold.
- **BUY-008 (upload).** WHEN the owner of an accepted live request uploads proof THE SYSTEM SHALL verify JPEG/PNG/WebP signature, exact bounded bytes, declared type and immutable publication through MEDIA before attaching it. A staging PUT, caller confirmation or upload failure SHALL never change money or mark payment verified. SVG, executable/polyglot mismatch, unsupported formats and oversized/empty objects SHALL fail safely.
- **BUY-009 (private evidence).** THE SYSTEM SHALL authorize every proof GET independently against the request, current real actor and purpose: own member or same-tenant front-office verifier only. General MEDIA member exposure, catalogue attachment, trainer staff-wide reads and platform preview SHALL NOT authorize it. Unknown, foreign, unexposed and forbidden ids SHALL share the same external refusal. Keys, ETags and URLs SHALL never enter member read models, notifications, analytics or audit.
- **BUY-010 (one proof).** WHEN proof is attached or replaced THE SYSTEM SHALL lock request/assets and preserve at most one active confirmed proof per request. A superseded or rejected proof SHALL remain immutable decision history, not regain active status; no asset SHALL be attached to two requests, even after cancellation or expiry. Replacement SHALL race safely with verification and preserve the verifier's exact viewed asset/revision.
- **BUY-011 (rejection).** WHEN staff rejects a request or proof THE SYSTEM SHALL require a trimmed reason of 3…200 characters labelled “shown to the member”, write actor/time/reason atomically and show it to the owner member. Rejecting proof returns the request to accepted, retaining its original expiry and permitting a fresh upload; it SHALL not renew holds indefinitely.
- **BUY-012 (record).** WHEN the verifier confirms externally received funds against the currently active proof and accepted revision THE SYSTEM SHALL serialize request, proof, retry and existing ledger/resource locks, record actual amount/method/currency/actor via the existing money discipline and bind request/proof/payment/order or membership atomically. Failure of any guard, receipt, stock, entitlement or audit write SHALL roll everything back. A cash request may be recorded without a screenshot only with an explicit desk confirmation that cash was received; the member SHALL never be forced to fabricate a UPI screenshot.
- **BUY-013 (ledger reuse).** THE SYSTEM SHALL invoke unchanged `public.record_addon_sale(uuid,uuid,integer,uuid,uuid,timestamptz,timestamptz,payment_method,text,uuid)` as the real authenticated staff caller for exact-price add-ons (existing initial PT slot rules included), and preserve its complete result `(order_id,payment_id,initial_session_id,replayed)`. Renewal SHALL insert an ordinary manual paid payment under existing RLS/triggers, not call a nonexistent `record_payment` RPC or hand-extend dates. No definer escalation SHALL impersonate staff to bypass ledger guards.
- **BUY-014 (mismatch).** WHEN actual received amount/currency differs from the accepted request THE SYSTEM SHALL show both exact values and the difference, never silently adjust order totals, tax or accepted terms. Existing add-on sale requires exact current offer total, so mismatched funds SHALL NOT activate that order; resolution is desk-assisted under the owner choice below. Renewal partial amounts SHALL retain existing cumulative-grant behavior and SHALL not promise a whole period until the ledger grants it.
- **BUY-015 (binding).** THE SYSTEM SHALL enforce tenant-composite FKs, unique request→payment and payment→request binding, unique proof→request and proof→payment causal binding, and immutable bound actor/amount/currency/target/revision/time. Binding SHALL independently verify same tenant/member, real seller, arrived payment and exact order relationship, not trust client ids. Cross-tenant/unknown payment ids SHALL reveal no existence.
- **BUY-016 (replay).** WHEN the same actor retries the same UUID command with exactly normalized original facts THE SYSTEM SHALL return the original result read-only, without additional payment, receipt, stock effect, period, pack, proof decision or audit. Changed facts/actor under that key SHALL conflict; late network results SHALL not overwrite a newer screen state. Current terminal history SHALL be returned without reopening purchase state.
- **BUY-017 (refund).** WHEN a linked payment is refunded/reversed THE SYSTEM SHALL preserve recorded request/proof/payment history and display existing completed-return truth separately. Requested/processing refunds SHALL not display returned money; PAY SHALL not refund, restock, reset proof reuse, or change renewal dates independently. Original paid fields, ADD terminal states and return ceilings remain unchanged.
- **BUY-018 (bounds).** THE SYSTEM SHALL enforce draft `BUY_LIMITS`: request TTL 24 hours from acceptance (unaccepted request TTL 24 hours from creation), 5 open requests/member, 10 creations/member/rolling day, 10 proof registrations/member/rolling hour, and existing 60 media registrations/tenant/hour shared aggregate. Reasons 3…200; quantity at most SHP's 10; lists clamp to `MEMBER_PAGE_SIZE_DEFAULT`. MEDIA's 2 MiB cap, 300-second upload TTL and 12-byte signature head are reused. Private proof GET TTL SHALL be 60 seconds; no bearer URL is represented as instantly revocable. Failed/replayed commands SHALL not farm counters or reset deadlines.
- **BUY-019 (database defense).** THE SYSTEM SHALL protect every new table with tenant-claim RLS and indexed tenant/member/request lookup columns, revoke all anon and authenticated DML, deny direct proof metadata SELECT, and expose only safe read RPCs. Table invariants SHALL bind trusted writers too. Authenticated RPC EXECUTE does not imply business permission; every command revalidates actors before target lookup. No raw claim text cast SHALL create an existence oracle.
- **BUY-020 (audit).** WHEN a material transition succeeds THE SYSTEM SHALL atomically append `purchase_request.created`, `.accepted`, `.quote_reconfirmed`, `.rejected`, `.cancelled`, `.expired`, `.recorded`, and `payment_proof.attached`, `.superseded`, `.rejected`, `.bound` as applicable. Audit includes ids, before/after state, exact amount/currency, decision actor and reason; never screenshot content, UPI ids, account details, object key/URL or invented historical attribution. Audit failure aborts the action; replays append nothing.
- **BUY-021 (offline).** WHILE offline THE SYSTEM SHALL show only identity-scoped last-good in-memory read data with stale timestamp; commands/upload SHALL require connection, show a clear error, and never queue or claim success. Sign-out, rebinding and tenant/member change SHALL clear data and proof URLs. Private proof bytes SHALL not be persisted by the app; responses SHALL use no-store.
- **BUY-022 (copy).** THE SYSTEM SHALL label upload “Pending verification”; only genuine bound ledger receipt permits “Payment recorded”. It SHALL say the gym checks received money, never “bank verified”, “payment successful” after upload, or imply automatic settlement. Recorded renewal SHALL show actual resulting membership dates, including no extension where existing rules so determine.
- **BUY-023 (retention).** THE SYSTEM SHALL separate private object deletion from immutable causal metadata. Draft default: delete proof objects 90 days after their verification/rejection/supersession decision; for undecided abandoned proof use request closure/expiry as clock. Preserve minimal linked request/proof tombstone metadata under existing eight-year financial/audit clock and longer while retained immutable FKs require it. No cascade, SET NULL, financial-history rewrite or blanket MEDIA 30-day released-object rule SHALL erase proof causality. Bound objects under explicit legal hold SHALL be excluded with documented release/expiry. Legal review/field map remains owner-gated; no DPDP-compliance claim is made.
- **BUY-024 (delivery).** WHEN a request is accepted, rejected, proof rejected or payment recorded THE SYSTEM SHALL offer deduped transactional NTF events without screenshot content or money/account details on lockscreen. Existing consent/guardian-device routing and quiet-hour defaults SHALL remain authoritative. Push failure SHALL not undo durable request truth.
- **BUY-025 (surface).** THE SYSTEM SHALL provide member detail/list, desk approval/verifier queue, receipt links and all loading/empty/error/stale/permission/validation/conflict/pending/success states using Chalkline, exact existing money formatter, light/dark, 200% text, reduced motion and accessible touch targets. Verifier confirmation SHALL disclose request price, actual received amount, method/currency, currently viewed proof and resulting action before commit.

## Proposed public boundary (names are draft, not exports yet)

Database: `public.purchase_request_kind` = `shop`, `pt`, `renewal`; `public.purchase_request_status` and `public.payment_proof_status` as BUY-005. `purchase_requests`: tenant/member/target ids, creation key and normalized facts, snapshot jsonb, accepted revision, accepted/expiry/closure timestamps and actors, status, active proof id, immutable recorded payment/order/membership link. `payment_proofs`: tenant/request/media ids, creator member/Auth id, immutable upload/decision timestamps, disposition, decision actor/reason, bound payment id and object purge/hold timestamps. Every money snapshot includes amount and currency; renewal historic GST unavailable remains null, never invented. Named composite keys/FKs and partial unique active-proof index required. New enums are generated through CLI, never handwritten TS status constants.

Proposed authenticated-only, empty-path RPCs (PUBLIC/anon revoked): `create_purchase_request(p_request_key uuid,p_kind purchase_request_kind,p_target_id uuid,p_quantity integer,p_expected_revision uuid)`, `accept_purchase_request(p_request_id uuid,p_expected_revision uuid,p_command_key uuid)`, `reconfirm_purchase_quote(p_request_id uuid,p_expected_revision uuid,p_command_key uuid)`, `cancel_purchase_request(p_request_id uuid,p_command_key uuid)`, `reject_purchase_request(p_request_id uuid,p_expected_revision uuid,p_reason text,p_command_key uuid)`, `attach_payment_proof(p_request_id uuid,p_asset_id uuid,p_expected_revision uuid,p_command_key uuid)`, `reject_payment_proof(p_request_id uuid,p_asset_id uuid,p_expected_revision uuid,p_reason text,p_command_key uuid)`. Commands return safe request detail plus `replayed boolean`, decimal text amounts, no storage fields. `read_member_purchase_requests(p_limit integer,p_after_created_at timestamptz,p_after_id uuid)` and `read_purchase_requests` with same pagination return safe own-member/front-office rows ordered `(created_at desc,id desc)`; `read_purchase_request(p_request_id uuid)` shares actor filtering. Mutations other than recording are narrow definers; recording is an invoker orchestration calling guarded private binding only after ledger writes. Exact recording signature/renewal revision token must freeze after choices below; inventing a plan `quote_version` is forbidden (plans have none).

Proof media extension: add `payment_proof` to non-status `MediaKind`/DB kind check, distinguish exactly one staff/member creator, preserve existing kinds' authorization and trigger guarantees; dedicated registration/finalization must prove live owned accepted request. Existing generic member-photo URL SHALL reject proof kind. Extend the existing Edge boundary with `proof-confirm` and `proof-url`, verifying caller JWT and request exposure before service-only private metadata reads/finalization. Staging `<tenant>/staging/payment_proof/<uuid>.<ext>`, immutable private publication `<tenant>/published/payment_proof/<uuid>.<ext>`; server generates keys. Conditional ETag copy, length/type/magic verification, no client PUT at published key, attach transaction and deletion tombstones reuse MEDIA. Do not broaden existing public catalogue signer or staff photo readers. Approval of this new member creator/private-proof boundary is required as part of the PAY contract; prior MEDIA approval alone does not authorize it.

Web/native transport: session-before-body strict JSON; `/api/member/purchase-requests` POST/GET; `/api/member/purchase-requests/[id]` GET; `/cancel`, `/reconfirm`, `/proof-upload-url`, `/proof-confirm` POST; `/api/purchase-requests/[id]/accept`, `/reject`, `/reject-proof`, `/record` POST and `/api/purchase-requests` GET for staff. `/api/purchase-requests/[id]/proof-url` POST authorizes member or real verifier and supplies no-store GET <=60s. Responses use the existing envelope. Invalid shape→400 `invalid_request`; absent/foreign/forbidden id→404 `request_unavailable`; stale revision/terminal/expiry/quote/stock→409 stable named refusal; format/type→422 `upload_rejected`; limit→429 `rate_limited`; storage/unknown commit→500 retryable generic failure using the original key. Existing ledger GL refusals remain intact and are mapped through existing parsers; reserve no new GL number until current registry is checked at freeze. Auth/scope failure→403 `not_permitted`, no target detail. Native uploads require existing package capability audit before adding a file-picker dependency.

## Only necessary owner choices before freeze

1. **Stock contract:** F10 says acceptance reserves stock; approved SHP deliberately has soft derived holds that ordinary counter sales can exhaust. Recommended smallest choice: explicitly retain soft holds, disclose collection can fail, and use SHP locks/expiry; if owner requires a hard guarantee, approve narrowly extending existing stock/sale guards so counter sales respect accepted holds. New independent money tests are mandatory. Never implement a second inventory or pretend soft hold is guaranteed.
2. **Mismatched funds:** unchanged add-on sale cannot record arbitrary actual amount on its exact-price order. Recommended: an independent ordinary received-money row with no entitlement, bound to a mismatch decision, then desk refund/replacement under existing rules; request cannot be marked fulfilled/recorded-as-purchase until exact sale reconciliation is approved. If owner requires one payment to activate the original offer at a different price, this is a money-spec amendment, not a PAY wrapper. Freeze distinct mismatch state/binding signature only after this decision; current proposed status list deliberately does not fake resolution.
3. **Current-plan renewal price:** current membership sold terms are frozen once any money arrived; renewal ledger uses sold net price/duration, while PLC displays today's price and retired memberships cannot extend. Recommended smallest scope: renew only active/frozen eligible held membership at its sold terms, explicitly distinguish today's catalogue price and route retired/deactivated-plan/changed-plan purchases to desk; owner must approve this departure from implied current-price confirmation. A new successor membership/current-price renewal needs an explicit membership-creation contract amendment, with no rewrite of paid history.

90-day proof-object deletion is F10's proposed engineering default, not another approval for unchanged behavior. Qualified legal sign-off/field-map approval remains a release prerequisite, not a claim this draft can grant. The exact proof creator/Edge permission extension freezes with PAY; no unrelated infrastructure approval is requested.
