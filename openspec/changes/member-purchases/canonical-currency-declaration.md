# PAY canonical currency representation — FROZEN

2026-10-04. Mechanical reconciliation of existing MNY-001/MNY-002 without
changing approved BUY-014 or existing payment guards. Accepted purchase quotes
and fulfilled v1 sales remain INR. No new money unit, conversion, payment,
price, authority or fulfillment rule is introduced.

`public.purchase_requests` carries a canonical, non-null `currency` text
column. It is generated from `coalesce(recorded_currency, 'INR'::text)` and
cannot be supplied or changed directly. Before payment recording the quote
currency is INR under the frozen contract; `recorded_currency` and
`recorded_amount_paise` retain their nullable not-yet-recorded factual meaning.
After recording the canonical currency agrees with the actual recorded currency,
including a Shop/PT currency mismatch permitted by BUY-014 through the existing
authenticated money guards. Such a mismatch grants no purchase entitlement.
Existing currency/amount/recorded-state checks remain in force. Do not fill
absent recorded facts, alter snapshots, convert amounts or add a fulfillment
currency choice to satisfy the global schema invariant.

The retained WSP attempt contract analogously gives
`notification_whatsapp_attempts.currency` a non-null generated alias of
`hold_currency`. Preserve existing INR, hold, movement and replay constraints;
there is no additional spend or currency mutation command.

The existing independent global money suites remain unchanged. Independent
visible and holdout supplements derive semantic checks from this declaration
before the separate implementation commit. They must cover not-yet-recorded
versus recorded truth, generated-column immutability, consistency, retained
non-INR offer/sale refusal and BUY-014 factual currency-mismatch recording
subject to existing payment guards. No money records change outside rollback-only fixtures.
Generated TypeScript declarations are regenerated by the CLI after CI applies
the approved migration; never hand-edit them.
