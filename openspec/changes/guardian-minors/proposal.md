# Guardian and minor protection (GRD-001…GRD-028)

Feature F7 of `docs/planning/v2-feature-map.md`, phase V2-A3 of `docs/planning/v2-campaign-goal.md`, batch 2
(`openspec/changes/v2-batch2-shared/shared.md`: migration `20261003090000_guardian_minors.sql`, tests
`69_guardian_minors.sql` / `h69_guardian_minors_holdout.sql`, SQLSTATEs `GL083`–`GL085`, ADR-178). Depends on the
frozen contract of `openspec/changes/member-invites` (INV, including its v1.1 amendments); read it first, this file
states only the minor deltas. `staff-invites` (STI) is untouched by this change.
Rigor: **full blind arrangement (ADR-059)** for the database objects, the replaced INV / comms / scan
functions and the API routes — the silent mistakes here are a child scored for absence without a lawful
basis, a stranger linked to a child's membership, and a message sent to the child instead of the guardian.
Full blind also applies to visible app and holdout app suites, written by separate implementation-blind authors. The screens use one implementer after all batch-2 contracts and bars are frozen.

## Quality bar

The bar document is `docs/design/v2/grd-bar.md`, written by the bar author before any test (Gauntlet step 1; this
proposal deliberately contains no bar evidence it did not fetch). Two references were located on 2026-10-02 and
are fetched and documented in the bar; public documentation is evidence of documented behaviour, not native UI proof: the statute the feature answers to —
Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, section 9 (children: 9(1) verifiable consent of the parent or lawful
guardian before processing; 9(3) no tracking or behavioural monitoring of children; official Gazette copy `https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023-1.pdf`)
— and a product precedent for parent-consented supervision of a minor's account, Google Family Link ("Set up
supervision", consent is the parent's, consequences and limits are stated) at `https://support.google.com/families/answer/9499456`.
The bar author may add others (a consumer fitness or studio app's under-18 sign-up, a school-fee or kids-club
guardian record) and tags anything the public docs are silent on **[own]**.

Structural criteria for the blind critic, measurable: (1) every guardian or consent state names its consequence
for absence follow-ups in one sentence (the seven `GUARDIAN_SCORING_COPY` strings, verbatim); (2) recording or
withdrawing consent takes at most three interactions from an open member page (confirm checkbox, enter source, record; the known-minor form is inline, with no disclosure or preliminary Record consent button);
(3) the 18th-birthday handover is behind a confirm panel with a required reason and says exactly what ends (the
guardian's sign-in to this membership) and what does not (visits, payments, history); (4) status is dot plus word
("Under 18", "Consent on record") never colour alone; (5) no copy blames the child, the guardian or the desk and none
shows an SQLSTATE; (6) the `link_email_in_use` hint names no other member; (7) light and dark, 44 px targets, large
text, reduced motion; (8) the section never appears for trainers or members.

## Why

ADR-172 recorded two open DPDP items before obligations commence: a guardian-consent record for under-18 members
and a decision on whether absence-risk scoring may run for them. Today `members.date_of_birth` exists, nothing
reads it, the no-show scan scores every member with a live membership, the privacy page says FitCruxx has "no
guardian-consent form of its own yet", WhatsApp links go to the member's own phone, and INV (ADR-176 point 8)
invites whatever address is on the member row. The owner's decision for F7 is fail-safe: scoring is on only when
the member is known to be an adult, or is a known minor with a guardian on file and a current guardian consent;
a minor's app access links the guardian's Google account; contact goes to the guardian; attendance is always
recorded. D1 (owner decision 2026-10-02): one Google account is exactly one member, ever — so the same
guardian's Gmail binds at most one child's row, and the gym chooses another account for the next child at invite time.

In: DB (enum, five `members` columns, one append-only table, fourteen `app` helpers, three triggers, seven public
commands/readers, one guarded organization-settings timestamp and its trigger, replaced INV `issue`/`redeem`/`peek`, replaced `app.run_no_show_scan`, two exact-delta edits to
the Phase 6 WhatsApp path), shared contracts, web (member-page `GuardianPanel`, four routes, one-time attestation banner, coverage note on the
red list, attention page, privacy sentence), audit, docs. Out (recorded, not built): any mobile surface; guardian
self-service consent or withdrawal from the guardian's own app session; a verifiable-consent mechanism beyond
staff attestation (for example an OTP to the guardian's phone) — a legal question, see "Contract questions"; a
second guardian per member; refusing or warning on a date of birth under 13 (the privacy page says "not for
children under 13", ADR-172 kept 13+ as an owner decision); a minor badge on the roster, check-in screen or
messages list; suppression of promotional or motivation messages to minors; email delivery to the guardian
(the `email` channel is unbuilt); automatic reminders before an 18th birthday; the DPDP erasure routine itself
(this change only declares the new columns to its field inventory); platform-console views of guardian data;
**the payer note on receipts (decided OUT, GRD-028)**.

## Bar decisions and deliberate deviations

- **Eligibility is derived at read time from stored facts; no stored flag.** A cached `scoring_enabled` would desync on the first date rollover or edit. One function answers every caller (GRD-003).
- **Missing DOB is fail-safe off unless covered by the owner’s one-time legacy attestation** (authoritative batch-2 decisions, GRD-002). No DOB is invented. The immutable timestamp covers only missing-DOB members created on or before it; later creations and known minors do not qualify. Until attested the one-time banner and coverage note explain the consequence.
- **Guardian consent lives in a new append-only table, not in `consents`** (the owner left the choice open). Reasons: `consent_purpose` is pinned as exactly `marketing`/`service` by `09_comms_structure` and `h09_comms` (an added label breaks two immutable suites, one of them the holdout); the console's consent form and `/api/consents` enumerate every label, so a third purpose would surface as a communications toggle; and a guardian's consent must be bound to *which* guardian gave it, which a `consents` row cannot carry. The new table keeps everything DPD-002…004 ask of `consents`: versioned, timestamped, purpose-stated (the table's single purpose is absence follow-up), withdrawal is a new row, history is never deleted.
- **Consent is bound to the guardian's identity by a snapshot.** Each row stores the guardian name and relation as they stood. If the guardian's name or relation later changes, the standing consent is `stale` and scoring is off until a new consent is recorded (a typo fix in the name also needs re-recording — deliberately conservative; phone and email changes do not void it).
- **"Guardian record complete" means name, relation and phone.** Email is needed only to invite. A minor with a complete record but no guardian email can be tracked (with consent) but cannot be invited.
- **For a known minor the invite address is `guardian_email`** — one field serving as both the guardian's contact address and the Google account that links. For a second child of the same guardian the gym types the address of the account that should link (their own, or the other parent's) into that child's guardian email field; INV's refusal copy (`account_already_linked`) covers mistakes and the read model warns in advance (`link_email_in_use`). A separate "login address" column is a later option if email delivery ever needs the two to differ (Contract question 7).
- **INV's issue/redeem/peek are replaced with the smallest possible delta** (GRD-014/015): the comparison address and one extra "bindable" conjunct; every other byte, outcome, audit shape and SQLSTATE order is INV's. `read_member_app_access` is deliberately not replaced (GRD-014).
- **The pre-auth accept page keeps INV's generic notice** — `peek_member_invite` may return no other column (INV-012), so the page cannot know it is a guardian invite. The guardian is told by the gym's share message (`guardianInviteShareMessage`).
- **The account handover at 18 is an explicit owner/manager command, never automatic** (feature map F7), enabled only for accounts linked through the guardian path (`guardian_linked_at`), so it can never unlink an adult's own account by mistake.
- **A closed case is closed, not deleted** — NSH-005's reasoning: the follow-ups are the record that somebody tried. Cases are closed the moment eligibility is lost (GRD-009), not at the next nightly scan, because "consent withdrawal stops scoring" is false if the work queue keeps a minor on it for up to a day.
- **Payer note on receipts: OUT** (GRD-028).

Authoritative batch-2 decisions override stale shared.md text, including full-blind app tests, the legacy cutoff, named Phase-6 comms replacements and the single legal integration pass.

## EARS requirements

- **GRD-001 (minor, adult, unknown).** THE SYSTEM SHALL treat a member as a *known minor* on date D when `date_of_birth` is non-null and D is before `app.member_adult_on(date_of_birth)`, as a *known adult* when it is non-null and D is on or after that date, and as *age unknown* when it is null. D is the gym's calendar date (`organizations.timezone` — never UTC, never a branch timezone). `member_adult_on` is the 18th anniversary of the birth date, inclusive; for a 29 February birth it is 1 March of the 18th year (the later day — fail-safe). A date of birth after D is a known minor. Boundary table, to be tested exactly: born 2008-03-01 is a minor on 2026-02-28 and an adult on 2026-03-01; born 2008-02-29 is a minor on 2026-02-28 and an adult on 2026-03-01; born 2000-01-01 is an adult on 2018-01-01; born 2026-10-03 is a minor on 2026-10-02; null is unknown on any date; for a gym in `Asia/Kolkata` at the instant 2026-03-01 19:00 UTC the gym date is 2026-03-02, so born 2008-03-02 is an adult (UTC would say minor).
- **GRD-002 (legacy attestation and fail-safe).** WHEN `date_of_birth` is null THE SYSTEM SHALL use `on_adult` iff the gym’s `members_without_dob_attested_adult_at` is non-null and `members.created_at <=` that timestamp; otherwise it SHALL use `off_age_unknown`. No attestation means literal fail-safe off. A known minor SHALL never qualify via attestation, including after a DOB edit. Attestation SHALL change no age fact, invite/contact/check-in rule or stored DOB. WHEN a real active non-impersonating gym owner confirms the one-time attestation THE SYSTEM SHALL lock the settings row, set the timestamp once and audit it; concurrent/repeated calls SHALL return the original timestamp without further writes/audit. All other roles and direct authenticated writes SHALL be refused. Existing cases for ineligible members close at the first scan; attestation restores eligibility at the next scan without reopening old closed cases.
- **GRD-003 (one gate, seven states).** THE SYSTEM SHALL decide eligibility for any inference over a member's attendance in exactly one place, `app.member_scoring_state(tenant, member, on)`, never from a stored flag: `off_age_unknown` (age unknown and not covered by GRD-002); `on_adult` (known adult or GRD-002 attested legacy adult); for a known minor: `off_no_guardian` (guardian record incomplete — name, relation and phone not all present), else by consent state `off_no_consent` (`none`), `off_consent_withdrawn` (`withdrawn`), `off_consent_stale` (`stale`), `on_consent` (`granted`). Consent state from the newest `guardian_consents` row for the member, ordered `recorded_at desc, id desc`: no row → `none`; newest `granted = false` → `withdrawn`; newest `granted = true` whose stored guardian name (case-insensitive) and relation equal the member's current ones → `granted`; newest `granted = true` otherwise → `stale`. `app.member_scoring_eligible` is true exactly for `on_adult` and `on_consent`, and false for an unreadable member or a null date. Attendance rows of a member are read by no scoring path without this gate (GRD-018).
- **GRD-004 (guardian record).** THE SYSTEM SHALL let any member carry a guardian (name, relation from the enum, phone, email) at any age, every field optional, name and relation together (`members_guardian_identity_chk`), phone and email only with a name (`members_guardian_contact_chk`), phone E.164, email plausible. The same guardian contact fields SHALL be storable on any number of members of a gym (siblings); no uniqueness exists on them.
- **GRD-005 (set age and guardian).** WHEN real front-office staff (owner, manager, front desk; never an impersonation or preview identity) call `set_member_age_guardian` THE SYSTEM SHALL, atomically, set the member's `date_of_birth` and guardian columns to the arguments after trimming every text argument and turning an empty string into null. Order of refusals: `42501` (not front office, impersonating, or the member is not visible in the caller's gym — indistinguishable) → `22023` (null member id; `p_date_of_birth` later than `app.gym_today`, message containing `date_of_birth_in_future`; a guardian relation, phone or email given without a name) → `23514` from the checks above. All-null guardian arguments clear the guardian (name, relation, phone, email) and leave `guardian_linked_at` alone. A call that changes nothing SHALL write nothing (no `updated_at` bump, no audit row). The command never writes `user_id`, status, or any other column.
- **GRD-006 (guardian consent record).** WHEN real front-office staff call `record_guardian_consent` for a *known minor* with a *complete* guardian record THE SYSTEM SHALL lock the member row, then append one `guardian_consents` row carrying `granted`, the trimmed `version` and `source`, a **server-copied snapshot** of the member's current `guardian_name` and `guardian_relation`, the acting staff id, and `recorded_at = greatest(clock_timestamp(), previous + 1 microsecond)`, and return `changed = true`. Order of refusals: `42501` (role, impersonation, unknown or foreign member) → `22023` (null `p_granted`; `p_version` not matching `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}$`; `p_source` blank or over 200 after trimming) → `GL084` (age unknown or known adult) → `GL083` (guardian record incomplete). An exact repeat is a no-op returning the existing row with `changed = false` and writing nothing: the newest row has the same `granted` and `version` and, when granted, a snapshot equal to the current guardian. Rows are never updated or deleted by any session (INT-001 reasoning; DPD-002…004).
- **GRD-007 (withdrawal).** WHEN `record_guardian_consent` records `granted = false` THE SYSTEM SHALL, in the same transaction, close every live case (GRD-009) and the member's state SHALL read `off_consent_withdrawn` immediately. Withdrawal removes nothing: the consent history, the closed cases and their follow-ups, and every attendance row remain.
- **GRD-008 (the scan).** THE SYSTEM SHALL change `app.run_no_show_scan(p_tenant_id, p_today)` by exactly two predicates and no other byte of the `20260909190000` body: in the **close pass** the case-closing condition `not exists (<live membership>)` becomes `(not exists (<live membership>) or not app.member_scoring_eligible(p_tenant_id, c.member_id, v_today))`; in the **open pass** the line `where c.absent_days > v_threshold` gains `and app.member_scoring_eligible(p_tenant_id, c.member_id, v_today)` immediately after it. The evaluation date is the scan's own `v_today` (so the `p_today` test hook controls age arithmetic too). A closed case has `status = 'closed'` and `closed_at` set, `returned_at` untouched, follow-ups kept; the return value remains the number of cases **opened**; `public.run_no_show_scan_all`, the Edge Function and the red-list view are unchanged. An adult with a date of birth behaves exactly as before.
- **GRD-009 (immediate closure).** WHEN a write to `members` changes `date_of_birth`, `guardian_name`, `guardian_relation`, `guardian_phone` or `guardian_email`, or `record_guardian_consent` records a withdrawal, THE SYSTEM SHALL call `app.close_ineligible_cases(tenant, member, cause)` in the same transaction: if `app.member_scoring_eligible(tenant, member, app.gym_today(tenant))` is false it closes the member's live cases (`open`, `contacted`, `follow_up_due`) exactly as GRD-008 does and, when at least one closed, writes `member.scoring_stopped` with `after = {closed_cases: n, cause}` where cause is `consent_withdrawn`, `age_changed` (the date of birth changed) or `guardian_changed` (any other watched column). It opens nothing: gaining eligibility waits for the next scan. Recorded residual: a scan that is mid-run while a withdrawal commits can leave one case open until the next scan's close pass (≤ 1 gym day).
- **GRD-010 (attendance and everything else is never gated).** THE SYSTEM SHALL record attendance for every member regardless of age state, guardian record or consent — QR, assisted, staff-assisted RPC and the member mobile path — and SHALL leave membership, payment, renewal, add-on, streak and check-in-gate behavior unchanged. A minor with no guardian can check in, be sold a membership and be invoiced; only the absence-risk inference and the contact/link routing below change.
- **GRD-011 (turning 18).** WHEN the gym-local date reaches `member_adult_on` THE SYSTEM SHALL need no write: the member's scoring state flips to `on_adult` by date (the next scan opens cases if warranted), contact routing (GRD-017) and the invite address (GRD-014) revert to the member's own, the guardian columns and every consent row are retained untouched, and a guardian-linked account stays linked until the explicit handover (GRD-012). ## ADR-178 text

**ADR-178 — Guardian and minor protection: eligibility is derived, consent is bound to the guardian, a minor's app access links the guardian's account (owner-approved feature map F7 and D1, 2026-10-02).** Implements the guardian data and gate contract from ADR-172; qualified legal review remains unresolved. Decisions, each with the alternative rejected. (1) *One gate, derived at read time.* `app.member_scoring_state` answers every question about scoring from `date_of_birth`, the guardian record and the newest guardian consent; no stored flag. Rejected: a `scoring_enabled` column, which desyncs on every date rollover and edit. (2) *Fail-safe both ways.* Age unknown is off unless the one-time owner-audited legacy timestamp covers created_at on or before its cutoff; a known minor needs a complete guardian record (name, relation, phone) and a current consent; a known adult is on. No attestation is automatic; the banner and coverage note persist until the owner attests. New missing-DOB members remain fail-safe off. No DOB is invented, and a known minor never qualifies via the cutoff. (3) *A new append-only table, not a new `consent_purpose` label.* The vocabulary is pinned by two immutable suites, enumerated by the console and `/api/consents`, and cannot carry which guardian consented; `guardian_consents` stores a snapshot of the guardian's name and relation, and a change of either voids the standing consent until it is re-recorded (`stale`). Rejected: extending the enum. (4) *Guardian fields live on `members`* as the feature map says (one column-list amendment to `01_tenancy_structure`); `guardian_email` doubles as the guardian's contact and the Google account that links, so a second child of the same guardian is given another address by the gym at invite time (D1); a separate login-address column is a later option. (5) *Withdrawal and data changes close cases immediately,* in the same transaction, and the nightly scan closes anything that slipped; closing keeps the follow-ups (NSH-005). A race with a mid-run scan leaves at most one case until the next close pass. Rejected: relying on the nightly scan alone, which leaves a withdrawn child on the work queue for up to a day, and a view predicate, which would not reach the metrics function that reads cases directly. (6) *INV is extended by deltas, not forked.* `issue`, `redeem` and `peek` are replaced with the address resolved by `app.member_invite_email` and one extra "guardian record complete" conjunct for known minors; every outcome, audit shape and SQLSTATE order is INV's, `peek` still returns only the gym name, and the pre-auth notice stays generic. Two INV holdout scenarios that asserted minors are not specially blocked are amended, as ADR-176 point 8 anticipated. (7) *The handover at 18 is explicit and scoped:* an owner/manager command, available only for accounts linked through the guardian path, deleting the guardian's sessions, retaining the guardian fields and consent history; nothing is automatic. (8) *Contact routes to the guardian* through one resolver used at the four places the WhatsApp path reads a member's phone; a known minor with no complete guardian gets no recipient at all. (9) *Audit carries no personal values.* Rejected: putting dates of birth or guardian names in `audit_log`, which is retained 8 years under legal hold and would defeat erasure. (10) *No payer note,* no mobile surface, no guardian self-service, no email delivery. (11) *Legal posture.* DPDP s.9(3) prohibits behavioural monitoring of children without stating that consent cures it, and s.9(1) wants *verifiable* parental consent; staff attestation is the interim mechanism, legal review (ADR-168) is unresolved, and the minor branch of the gate is isolated so it can be disabled without a migration of data.

## Resolved decisions and recorded gaps
